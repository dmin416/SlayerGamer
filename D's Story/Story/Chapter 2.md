## Old Detroit

He walked. Gangs owned corners. Metro West looked stretched thin. OCP’s name sat on boards and rumors: Delta City plans, redevelopment talk, money with teeth. He learned it the civilian way: overheard news, shop talk, neighborhood fear. No investigator title. No special briefing.

*RoboCop world. Murphy is out there somewhere. I am a gardener with a gamer bag. Fine.*

## Dorothy Miller's Garden and Plant Shop

He found Dorothy Miller’s community garden and plant shop. She had run it for decades. Soil, seedlings, neighbors who needed green more than they needed speeches. Ard-draoi hands liked the work before his mouth caught up.

Dorothy sized him up without drama and let him help.

## Volunteer Work

He started unpaid: beds, watering, hauling, listening to residents. The garden gave him a reason to be outside that was not “tourist with a Challenge Token.”

## Paid Work as Gardening Assistant

Dorothy put him on paid work. Gardening assistant. Regular hours. Cash that was not gacha. He kept his head down and his system quieter in public.

## Robbery Near the Garden — Murphy and Lewis

A robbery hit near the garden. Alex Murphy and Anne Lewis responded. D gave a witness statement and went back to his own life. No badge. No ride-along.

The theft was one of several involving OCP components. Gangs were taking equipment on secret contracts. The responsible OCP executive was not identified yet.

## Settling In

Contact with Murphy and Lewis grew across later encounters. Gradual. Neighbor and witness territory, not a sudden detective partnership. D was not made a police investigator.

He kept gardening. He kept the Challenge Token unsnapped. Detroit stayed unfinished on purpose.

## Murphy Investigation / Car Bombing

Murphy’s investigation ran into a car bombing. Murphy was left critically injured. That injury is the path into the RoboCop program. D did not learn the program’s details here.

## Hospitalization

Hospital. Clara. David. D offered practical help over time and did not dig for classified answers. Murphy was alive, broken, and not yet the man in the metal.
