# Chapter 1: Horned Rabbit ID

Tutorial Instant Dungeon. Before Detroit. D alone.

## [The beginning]

He hit soft earth instead of concrete. Trees. Underbrush. The smell of damp leaf litter. No Detroit skyline. No apartment keys. Just a blue box and a quest that refused to wait.

```
[The beginning]
Objective – Observe a Horned Rabbit, kill one, and clear the dungeon
Reward – ID Create, ID Escape, 500 XP, $500, Random Skill Book
```

"Alright," D said, brushing dirt off his palms. "Observe first. Kill second. Clear third. Polaris?"

```
I am here. Inventory is unlocked. Use Observe on a living Horned Rabbit before you rush the first kill. Tutorial habits stick
```

He found white fur between the roots, small, horned, chewing something it should not have been chewing. Observe painted a name and a threat ranking over it. The rabbit did not care.

He killed it cleanly. Loot landed as harvest, not weapons: hide scraps, a small cut of meat, one cooked squirrel, and fifteen dollars. No spear. No club. The pattern was rabbit parts, and he filed that away.

```
Inventory updated
```

*Weapons would have been wrong anyway. This is food and material. Fine.*

## Horned Rabbits

White Horned Rabbits first. Then brown. He used Observe until the window felt normal, moved loot into inventory without thinking about pockets, and learned how XP ticked when something died.

Level 1 became Level 2 in the early fights. Ard-draoi grit and Arcanist tools did the work. No Slayer rage. No Blood Punch. No engineering. Bark Skin when something scored a hit. Aeromancy for space. Wild Shape when teeth and reach mattered more than hands. Primal Savagery rode that same transformation track; it was not a second ladder.

A hollow held four rabbits. After that clear he stood at Level 4, breathing hard, inventory heavier with hides, horns, bones, feet, meat, and more roasted squirrels.

```
Level Up
```

"Hollow cleared. Still not the end of the dungeon."

```
Correct. Keep going
```

## Rare Pan / Mana Strike

Somewhere in the grind he found a pan: ordinary metal, exceptionally well made. No enchantment. No skill stamped on the rim. Rare quality, nothing more.

```
Item Acquired
Rare Pan
```

*Not The Butcher. Not a cleaver. A pan. Polaris has a sense of humor, or the loot table does.*

Cooking would come from practice. So would blunt work if he swung it. The pan granted neither.

In the same stretch of fights he learned he could push mana into a strike. Fist, pan, or a Wild Shape bite. Extra force. Extra cost. The system labeled it.

```
Skill Gained
[Mana Strike] (1/100)
50 MP
Imbue a suitable attack with added energy. Works with weapons, unarmed strikes, or useful natural attacks in Wild Shape
```

"That stays," he said. "That definitely stays."

## [One Style to rule them all]

He decided, mid-dungeon, that he was not going to stay a pure spell-and-claw problem. He wanted a martial art that belonged to him: something he could grow for decades, not a borrowed form he forgot under pressure.

```
Quest added
[One Style to rule them all]
Objective – Develop a personal martial art
Status – Ongoing
```

"Foundation first. Fancy name later."

```
Noted
```

## Greater Horned Rabbit Alpha


The chief was larger, meaner, and done with tutorials. D used everything he had: Observe, Bark Skin, Absorb Elements when it mattered, Mana Strike on openings, Wild Shape when the horn came in low. He did not invent glory. He survived the fight.

```
Level Up
```

Level 4 became Level 9 when the chief fell. A Common Challenge Token dropped into inventory. He did not snap it.

```
Item Acquired
Common Challenge Token ×1
```

```
[The beginning] Complete
Rewards – ID Create, ID Escape, 500 XP, $500, Random Skill Book
```

The Random Skill Book resolved as Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist. One book. Not a second mystery volume beside it. He accepted the learn prompt. The book burned out of inventory.

```
Skill Learned
[Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist] (1/100)
Foundation for [One Style to rule them all]
```

Clear harvest filled the rest of the bag the way the opening notes recorded it: loins, saddles, haunches, livers, hearts, offal, fat, blood vials, stock and marrow bones, horns, pelts, feet, forage from the undergrowth, five Rare cooked rabbits, recipe scrap for Horned Rabbit Stock if he took it, a Lesser Beast Core if he took it, two minor health potions, five Common gacha tokens, and one Epic gacha token.

No goblin spears. No rabbit swords. No The Butcher.

## Clear Rewards / Chef Unlock

Passing Level 5 had unlocked a production-class slot. The system waited until after the chief and the quest payout to present it. He chose Chef.

```
Production Class Unlocked
Chef
Processing – prepare ingredients and carcasses
Preparing – turn them into dishes
```

Arcanist, Ard-draoi, and Chef would share one XP pool from here. Skills could still climb with practice.

```
Classes Updated
Arcanist 9/100
Ard-draoi 9/100
Chef 9/100
```

"Good. Food and fight both count."

```
Agreed. Do not eat the Challenge Token
```

"Was not going to."

Approved packs and books landed. Receipt was not use.

Currency at transfer: dungeon and quest cash only — **$590**.

He did not open every pack. He did not plant anything. He did not spend gacha. The staff in inventory did not finish [Look the Part].

```
Reminder
[Look the Part] remains active. Possession of a staff alone is insufficient
```

"I know. Companion and cloak still missing."

## Transfer to Detroit

The dungeon was done. Level 9. Chef unlocked. Challenge Token held. WSRSF at 1/100. Pan in the bag. Detroit waiting.

```
Tutorial Instant Dungeon complete. Transferring you to your lottery world
```

"RoboCop. Old Detroit. No Mad Max."

```
Correct. Stand ready
```

Polaris moved him. Horned Rabbit ID ended at the transfer. Chapter 2 began on the other side of that light.
