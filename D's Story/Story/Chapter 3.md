**Elsewhere, Claire.** Reach **Greenhouse Control Room** in the East Area. The sprawling **Plant 43** infestation and **Ivy** enemies obstruct the way. Take the **Dispersal Cartridge** from the control machine. The terminal uses unfamiliar symbol keys; the following number strings describe their *keypad positions*, not characters actually printed on screen in Arabic numerals: **3123** opens the greenhouse ladder shutter, and **2067** opens the **Drug Testing Lab** dispenser shutter, in this story. The clues are at the greenhouse ladder and on the **Trophy** found downstairs.

Proceed to **Drug Testing Lab** and operate the fluid-transfer machine until the left vial reaches the mark. The selected button solution is **red, green, blue, red, green, blue, red, green**. Take the cartridge holding the solution. Use the opened greenhouse ladder and move through the lower **Lounge** and **Low-Temperature Testing Lab** route. Retrieve the **Signal Modulator** and adjust it to the labeled circuit frequency **MURF**, lining its oscillation traces up before inserting it into the matching wall panel; restore power to open the Cold Testing Lab. Freeze the cartridge into **Herbicide**.

Return to the Greenhouse Control Room and run the dispersion sequence. The herbicide kills or disables plant growth and clears the route, but Ivies remain dangerous and some rise again. Retrieve the **Electronic Chip (Senior Staff)** from the dead researcher in the Greenhouse and upgrade the wristband. Optionally return to the Nap Room and tune the Signal Modulator to its own printed breaker frequency to open powered sleeping pods. A **Hip Pouch** and **Mr. Raccoon** are available in a pod. Collect any outstanding lab records and supplies before crossing the main shaft.

**D's laboratory progression** is affected by the herbicide dispersal and unlocked staff access. Do not make him recreate Plant 43 from an untouched starting state or solve the exact same unique vial puzzle again.


## Beat 19 — West Area: G-Virus and antivirus

**Elsewhere, Claire.** Use her upgraded wristband to access the **West Area** from the Main Shaft. Tune the Signal Modulator to **OSS** at the West Area panel to restore power. In **Biotesting Lab**, collect the **Lab Digital Video Cassette** and play it on the VCR in the nearby video room to learn more of William Birkin and Umbrella's attempts to seize his research. The cassette is **not** picked up from a body on the approach bridge.

Enter the **P-4 Level Testing Lab**. Claire reaches the antiviral storage and uses **Sherry's Pendant** to open the secured mechanism containing the **antiviral agent**. Take the agent and attempt to return to Sherry. **William, now G Stage 3**, attacks. Annette arrives, and Claire must protect the cure while escaping this encounter. The story of Umbrella's theft and William's self-injection emerges through these scenes and the recorded evidence. Do not replace these with the 1998 game's original dialogue.

**D and Ada:** Their objective is a G-Virus sample, distinct from Claire's treatment for Sherry. Ada's claimed FBI identity becomes doubtful. D can recover a sample and confront Ada when he learns more, but no sample should appear magically if the player's actions prevented its acquisition.


## Beat 20 — G3, Sherry's treatment and evacuation alarm

**Elsewhere, Claire.** **G Stage 3** exposes vulnerable eyes on his arm, leg and back. Destroy these to open the eye cluster on his chest; attack that while he is vulnerable, evade his lunges and thrown objects, and repeat until William collapses. Claire returns with the antiviral agent. **Annette administers Sherry's treatment** and, fatally injured, shares her final moments with her daughter. Sherry survives. The NEST evacuation/self-destruct warning sounds. Claire obtains the clearance needed to travel onward with Sherry and begins the escape.

**The lab's two crises converge:** Default: Claire survives G3 while D is with Ada; Annette treats Sherry and dies. If **D is with Claire** for G3, they fight **together** — still one G3, no reset. Same evacuation alarm for everyone.


## Beat 21 — Escape train, G4 and Super Tyrant

**Elsewhere, Claire.** Head to the **Main Shaft** and descend toward the **laboratory's evacuation train**. Pass through the lower passages and save room while emergency warnings sound. Sherry helps Claire past a locked door as enemies close in. On the **train platform**, Claire obtains the **Joint Plug** from the train and installs it in the platform's **turntable control panel**. Collect the **Minigun from beside the platform controls**, not from an invented interior train control room.

**G Stage 4** attacks on all fours near the train. Claire uses the Minigun, focusing on the large collection of exposed eyes while avoiding charging attacks and leaps. Once the creature falls, Claire joins Sherry on the train as it prepares to depart.

**Simultaneously below the lab, D:** Mr. X reappears as Super Tyrant on the evacuation lift (D’s seat unless play regroups everyone). Claire fights **G4 once** on the platform (with D **together** if he is there; otherwise Claire + Sherry). Survivors meet on the same train for the shared stretch / G5.


## Epilogue — The train, G5 and daylight

The rear of the fleeing train shudders as William Birkin returns in his final mutation, G5. His enormous mass advances through the carriage. The three survivors have only one train and one chance to escape; whether D helps fight, uncouple the rear car or take another action is the player’s choice.

**The final shared event:** As the evacuation train departs, William returns in G5 form in the rear car. D, Claire and Sherry confront the danger together according to the player's decisions. The rear car is uncoupled, the train exits the doomed underground complex, and the survivors reach the road outside the city at daybreak. Claire may want word of Chris (entry hook only — not her character engine; see `Character\Claire.md`). Sherry's questions and D's response are left to live play.
