# Ard-draoi — Opening Story Breakdown

Authoritative opening continuity for D's story.

Chronological bullet-point plot notes. The opening is not required to fit into a fixed number of chapters. The events below are separated by story arcs, not mandatory chapter boundaries.

## 1. Death and character creation

- Duir Maqidev (D) dies after living 77 years, 77 days, 7 hours, 7 minutes, and 7 seconds and is selected by the multiversal Gamer lottery.
- He operates the system by thinking Start, registers his name, and chooses the random-race option.
- He receives Transcendent Human (Ard-draoi) and the Ard-draoi prestige class, with Nature Attunement, Grove Immortality, Knowledge of the Ancients, Primal Savagery, Druid Magic, and Eternal Cycle/Tribute.
- He makes a new body, physically about 21, 5'11", with black hair and green eyes.
- He chooses Mage → Arcanist as his first standard class. This replaces the Enchanter choice in the older prologue; the earlier text's Enchanting skill should not be silently counted as an additional starting Arcanist skill without an established unlock.
- Established starting skills from character creation: Primal Savagery (with Wild Shape as one transformation progression), Aeromancy, Druid Magic, Bark Skin, and Absorb Elements. Aeromancy is an Arcanist starting skill. Bark Skin and Absorb Elements are grove spells under the Ard-draoi/Druid Magic line, not Enchanter carryovers. Enchanting is not carried over. Further Arcanist techniques may still unlock through practice.
- He also receives Gamers Mind, Gamers Body, and Observe before the tutorial drop.
- He calls his system companion Polaris. Her helpful, mildly teasing manner carries over from the prologue.
- His lottery-assigned world is RoboCop, not Resident Evil. He must first complete the tutorial instance dungeon.
- The [Look the Part] quest is introduced: obtain an animal companion and assemble a fitting staff and cloak. It has no time limit and is not automatically completed by a reward appearing in inventory.

## 2. Horned Rabbit instance dungeon

- D enters the tutorial dungeon and receives [The beginning]: observe a Horned Rabbit, kill one, and clear the dungeon.
- He learns to use Observe, inventory, combat, loot and leveling while fighting Horned Rabbits. His opening advantages are Ard-draoi and Arcanist abilities, not Slayer rage, Blood Punch, Glory Kills, or engineering.
- The early encounters progress from ordinary White and Brown Horned Rabbits to stronger rabbits. The recorded progression is Level 1 → 2 in the first encounters and Level 2 → 4 after a hollow containing four rabbits.
- He obtains a Rare Pan during the dungeon. It is an ordinary, exceptionally well-made pan: no magic and no skill-granting property. Cooking and Blunt Melee are skills developed by use, not granted by the pan.
- D discovers Mana Strike: he imbues a suitable attack with added energy. It can work with a weapon, an unarmed strike, or any useful natural attack in Wild Shape.
- Wild Shape allows him to use Primal Savagery; they are part of one transformation capability, not independent level tracks.
- D receives the long-term quest [One Style to rule them all] after deciding to develop a personal martial art. It remains ongoing.
- D defeats the Greater Horned Rabbit Chief, advancing from Level 4 to Level 9.
- The chief awards a Common Challenge Token. D does not use it yet.
- Clearing [The beginning] awards ID Create, ID Escape, 500 XP, $500, and a Random Skill Book. The loot/reward reveal identifies that book as Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist. D accepts the prompt to learn it, consuming the book and unlocking the style at 1/100. This is one book, not a separate random book plus a second martial-arts book.

## 3. Production class and early rewards

- The level-up after defeating the chief reaches Level 9, and the system recognizes that D passed the Level 5 production-class unlock. It presents the class selection then; D chooses Chef.
- D's Arcanist, Ard-draoi, and Chef classes subsequently share one XP pool and level together. Individual skills can improve with practice.
- Chef includes Processing (preparing ingredients and carcasses) and Preparing (turning them into dishes).
- D receives his approved additional packs, books, equipment and gacha rewards. Receipt does not mean an item is opened, used, learned, planted, equipped, summoned or constructed. See the inventory section below.
- Once the dungeon is complete, Polaris transfers D directly into Detroit, his assigned RoboCop world. There is no Resident Evil transition inside the rabbit dungeon.

## 4. Detroit — arrival and early civilian life

- D awakens in a modest Detroit apartment, with basic furnishings, necessities, a lawful civilian identity, documentation and an apartment lease. He has no prearranged employment or OCP/police connections.
- He explores Old Detroit and gradually learns about the police department's struggle with violent gangs, OCP's influence, and the Delta City redevelopment plans.
- He finds Dorothy Miller's community garden and plant shop. Dorothy is an older neighborhood resident who has operated it for decades.
- D begins as a volunteer, helping with gardening and residents' needs. Dorothy later offers him paid work as a gardening assistant.
- While still a civilian, D encounters Alex Murphy and Anne Lewis when they respond to a robbery near the garden. He initially gives a witness statement and goes back to his own life.
- The incident is one of several thefts involving OCP components, with gangs secretly contracted to steal the equipment. The responsible OCP executive is not identified at the outset.
- D's contact with Murphy and Lewis grows gradually through subsequent encounters; he is not immediately made a police investigator.
- Murphy's investigation eventually leads to a car bombing, leaving him critically injured. This is the event leading to the RoboCop program in the combined setting.
- D visits Murphy in hospital, meets Clara and David, and offers the family practical help over time. He does not learn the details of the RoboCop project before Murphy returns to police duty.
- D discovers Murphy's transformation after RoboCop returns to active duty. The specific first reunion is not fixed in this outline.

## 5. Resident Evil 2 challenge during RoboCop

- Before the RoboCop storyline is finished, D snaps the Common Challenge Token earned from the Greater Horned Rabbit Chief.
- The token takes him to Resident Evil 2 Remake, beginning in a sedan outside the Mizoil gas station during the Raccoon City outbreak.
- The established RE2 campaign is followed from that point, including D's later experiences with Claire and Sherry. The RoboCop story is still unfinished when he makes this jump.
- The eventual return to Detroit and continuation of the Murphy/OCP story belong to the later plot, not the opening dungeon.

## 7. Character and mechanics continuity

- Classes: Arcanist (primary), Ard-draoi (prestige), Chef (production). All level from the same XP pool after unlocked.
- Class gains recorded in campaign summaries: Arcanist +5 INT/WIS/END per level; Ard-draoi +5 END/INT/WIS; Chef +5 STR/DEX and +2 LUCK. Racial perk bonuses and manually distributed points must also be accounted for in any full stat calculation.
- Primal Savagery/Wild Shape: one transformation progression, with Wild Shape allowing use of Primal Savagery.
- Starting Arcanist skill: Aeromancy. Starting grove skills: Druid Magic, Bark Skin, Absorb Elements. These remain established from character creation.
- Mana Strike: usable with any attack that benefits from additional imbued energy.
- One Style to rule them all: ongoing; Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist is only its foundation.
- Look the Part: ongoing until its actual completion conditions are met and recorded.

## 8. Later continuity — not opening-dungeon events

- D receives a Mr. X Companion Token through the RE2 challenge and activates it later in the RoboCop storyline. The summoned T-103 is Ty, distinct from the original Mr. X.
- A Slime Egg comes from Rare Box #8 and hatches later in the RoboCop storyline. Its later alchemical, biological and symbiote developments are not starter abilities.
- The Random Companion Coin above is a separate unused gacha result and does not duplicate Ty or the slime.
- Later benchmark levels (34 after RE2, 80 later in RoboCop) are not the opening dungeon's Level 9.
