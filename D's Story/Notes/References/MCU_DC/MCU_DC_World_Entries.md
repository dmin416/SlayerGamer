### 110. MCU
- **See:** Avengers Tower, Wakanda, Asgard, the Sanctum Sanctorum, Knowhere, Sakaar, Titan, Xandar, Vormir, the Quantum Realm
- **People:** Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, Spider-Man, Doctor Strange, Black Panther, the Guardians of the Galaxy, Thanos
- **Powers:** Super-soldier serum, the Mystic Arts, gamma mutation, spider powers, Thor's lightning, Scarlet Witch's chaos magic, Captain Marvel's cosmic energy
- **Items:** Infinity Stones, Iron Man suits, the Tesseract, the Eye of Agamotto, Pym Particles, vibranium
- **Weapons:** Mjolnir, Stormbreaker, Captain America's shield, the Infinity Gauntlet, the Ten Rings, Black Panther's suit, Hawkeye's trick arrows, web-shooters, Iron Man's repulsors

### 111. DC

- **People:** Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, the Flash, Aquaman, Green Lantern, Cyborg, the Joker, Lex Luthor, Darkseid
- **Powers:** Kryptonian powers, the Speed Force, Green Lantern willpower constructs, Amazon strength, Atlantean strength, Lazarus Pit resurrection
- **Items:** Green Lantern ring, Mother Boxes, Lasso of Truth, Batmobile, utility belt, Kryptonite
- **Weapons:** Batarangs, Green Lantern ring constructs, Wonder Woman's sword, shield and Bracelets of Submission, Aquaman's trident, Kryptonite spear
