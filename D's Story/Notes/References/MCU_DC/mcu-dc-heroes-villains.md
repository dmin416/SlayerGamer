# MCU and DC: Heroes and Villains

# MCU

## MCU Heroes

### Iron Man (Tony Stark)
- **Locations:** Stark Tower (Avengers Tower), the Malibu mansion, the Afghanistan cave, the Avengers Compound
- **Powers:** None (genius inventor)
- **Items:** Arc reactor, Iron Man suits (Mark I to Mark LXXXV), J.A.R.V.I.S. and F.R.I.D.A.Y., nanotech armor, Iron Legion drones
- **Weapons:** Repulsors, unibeam, micro-missiles, the Stark-built Infinity Gauntlet

### Captain America (Steve Rogers, Sam Wilson)
- **Locations:** Brooklyn, WWII Europe, Avengers Compound
- **Powers:** Super-soldier serum (Steve), Wakandan wing flight (Sam)
- **Items:** Wakandan vibranium wing suit, Redwing drone
- **Weapons:** Vibranium shield, Mjolnir (Steve proved worthy)

### Thor
- **Locations:** Asgard, New Asgard (Tønsberg, Norway), the Nine Realms, Nidavellir, Omnipotence City
- **Powers:** God of Thunder strength, lightning, longevity, Bifrost summoning
- **Items:** The Bifrost, Asgardian armor
- **Weapons:** Mjolnir, Stormbreaker

### Hulk (Bruce Banner)
- **Locations:** Sakaar, Culver University, gamma research labs
- **Powers:** Gamma strength and regeneration, Smart Hulk intellect
- **Items:** None
- **Weapons:** Sakaaran gladiator gear

### Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff, Yelena Belova)
- **Locations:** The Red Room, Budapest, the Red Room sky base
- **Powers:** Red Room conditioning
- **Items:** Red Guardian's suit, Taskmaster's mimicry tech
- **Weapons:** Widow's Bites

### Hawkeye (Clint Barton, Kate Bishop)
- **Locations:** Barton farm, New York
- **Powers:** None
- **Items:** Pym Particle arrowheads
- **Weapons:** Trick arrows (explosive, EMP, acid, Pym Particle)

### Spider-Man (Peter Parker)
- **Locations:** Queens, Midtown School of Science and Technology, the Multiverse
- **Powers:** Spider strength, agility, wall-crawling, Spider-Sense
- **Items:** Iron Spider suit, Karen AI, E.D.I.T.H. glasses
- **Weapons:** Web-shooters, Iron Spider legs

### Doctor Strange
- **Locations:** Kamar-Taj, the New York Sanctum Sanctorum, the Mirror Dimension, the Dark Dimension, Kamar-Taj's library
- **Powers:** The Mystic Arts, astral projection, portal sling-ring travel, spell shields
- **Items:** Eye of Agamotto (Time Stone), Cloak of Levitation, sling ring, Book of Vishanti
- **Weapons:** Eldritch whips, spell weapons

### Black Panther (T'Challa, Shuri)
- **Locations:** Wakanda (Birnin Zana, Warrior Falls, the Great Mound), the Ancestral Plane, Talokan
- **Powers:** Heart-Shaped Herb
- **Items:** Nanotech panther habit, Kimoyo beads, Vibranium, Royal Talon Fighter
- **Weapons:** Vibranium claws, Shuri's panther gauntlets, Dora Milaje vibranium spears

### Captain Marvel (Carol Danvers)
- **Locations:** Hala (Kree homeworld), the Kree Empire, Earth
- **Powers:** Tesseract cosmic energy, flight, photon blasts, Binary form
- **Items:** Quantum bands (Ms. Marvel's pair)
- **Weapons:** Photon blasts

### Ant-Man and the Wasp (Scott Lang, Hope van Dyne)
- **Locations:** San Francisco, the Quantum Realm, Pym's shrinking lab
- **Powers:** Size changing (Giant-Man included)
- **Items:** Ant-Man suit, Wasp suit, Pym Particles, Pym's shrinkable lab
- **Weapons:** Wasp's stingers, giant ants

### Scarlet Witch and Vision
- **Locations:** Westview, Sokovia, Mount Wundagore
- **Powers:** Chaos magic, reality warping, Mind Stone powers (Vision)
- **Items:** The Darkhold, the Mind Stone
- **Weapons:** Chaos magic hexes

### Guardians of the Galaxy
- **Locations:** Knowhere, Xandar, the Milano and the Benatar, Ego's planet, Counter-Earth
- **Powers:** Groot's regrowth, Drax's strength, Mantis's empathy, Nebula's cybernetics
- **Items:** The Orb (Power Stone), Quill's Walkman and Awesome Mix, Rocket's tech
- **Weapons:** Quill's element guns, Yondu's Yaka arrow, Gamora's Godslayer, Rocket's custom guns

### Shang-Chi
- **Locations:** Ta Lo, Macau, Ten Rings compound
- **Powers:** Chi martial arts
- **Items:** Dragon-scale armor
- **Weapons:** The Ten Rings

### The Eternals
- **Locations:** The Domo ship, Olympia, the Celestial Tiamut in the ocean
- **Powers:** Cosmic energy beams, super speed, matter transformation, mind control, illusions
- **Items:** The Uni-Mind
- **Weapons:** Cosmic energy weapons (Thena's weapons)

### Moon Knight
- **Locations:** London, Cairo, the Duat, Egyptian god temples
- **Powers:** Avatar of Khonshu (healing, strength)
- **Items:** Moon Knight and Mr. Knight suits, Ammit's ushabti scarab
- **Weapons:** Crescent darts, Khonshu's power

### Ms. Marvel (Kamala Khan)
- **Locations:** Jersey City, Karachi, the Noor dimension
- **Powers:** Hard-light constructs
- **Items:** The quantum bangle
- **Weapons:** Hard-light fists

### Fantastic Four (Earth-828)
- **Locations:** Baxter Building, retro-futurist New York of Earth-828
- **Powers:** Stretching (Reed), invisibility and force fields (Sue), flame and flight (Johnny), rock strength (Ben)
- **Items:** Fantasticar, H.E.R.B.I.E., the Excelsior ship
- **Weapons:** Sue's force fields, Johnny's nova flame

### Deadpool and Wolverine
- **Locations:** The Void, the TVA, Earth-10005
- **Powers:** Healing factors, Wolverine's adamantium skeleton
- **Items:** TVA TemPad
- **Weapons:** Adamantium claws

# DC

## DC Heroes

### Superman
- **Locations:** Metropolis, the Daily Planet, Smallville, the Fortress of Solitude, Krypton
- **Powers:** Flight, super strength, super speed, invulnerability, heat vision, freeze breath, X-ray vision, super hearing
- **Items:** Kryptonian crystals, the Kryptonian ship, the Fortress's Kryptonian AI
- **Weapons:** None

### Batman
- **Locations:** Gotham City, the Batcave, Wayne Manor, Arkham Asylum
- **Powers:** None (peak human, detective)
- **Items:** Batmobile, Batwing, Batsuits, utility belt, Bat-Signal
- **Weapons:** Batarangs, grapnel gun, Kryptonite spear, Hellbat armor

### Wonder Woman
- **Locations:** Themyscira, the WWI Western Front, Washington, D.C.
- **Powers:** Amazon strength, flight, divine heritage
- **Items:** Lasso of Truth, tiara, Golden Eagle armor
