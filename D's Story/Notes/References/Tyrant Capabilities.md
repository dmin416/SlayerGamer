# Tyrant capabilities

T-00. The remake calls him Mr. X while the power limiter is on. The four pictures in this folder are stages of that same body, not four different Tyrants.

This file is what the body can do. The station chase in Chapter 3 (RE2 Remake) adds a receiver and a faint leftover person. That addition is marked at the bottom. It does not replace the body.

## T-00 in the power limiter

The fedora, the trench coat and the gloves are the limiter. While they hold, the virus stays capped and he stays in the walking form. Umbrella's order in the station is to kill survivors. Later the same body is aimed at the G-virus sample.

- He is well over a man in height and built past any human frame. One punch puts a person down. A grab lifts them. He punches through masonry and through a jail-cell wall. Ben dies that way in the Leon-side route.
- He walks. The footsteps carry. He can lengthen the stride into a fast pursuit and a shoulder charge. He does not sprint while the coat is intact.
- Handguns, shotguns, the grenade launcher and a magnum stagger him and can drop him. He stands back up. A knife does not stop him. Fire staggers him. The station arsenal does not kill this form.
- Doors, stairs and the station layout are usable to him. A boarded door delays him until he breaks it. Loud noise pulls him: gunshots, and the clock-tower bell. Break his line of sight and stay quiet and he loses the immediate chase, then patrols until the next sound or sighting.
- He does not speak. He does not use guns. He does not negotiate.
- A van ram (Ada, behind the RPD) and falling debris knock him down. He comes back from both.
- William Birkin, in the G form, can tear this coated body apart. That happens in the elevator on Claire's route after the orphanage, and it is one of the few things that ends the coated Tyrant without the rocket. Guns still do not.

## Coatless body

Picture: `T-00 coatless.jpg`

The same frame with the limiter off and the mutation not yet spent. Gray hide, red fissures across the chest, shoulder and forearm, both hands still hands. This is the body the coat was holding down. Once the hide is exposed, the virus has a path to the clawed stages. Strength and durability are already past the coated cap. The walk becomes a hunt with fewer brakes.

## Arm mutation

Picture: `Mr X arm mutation.png`

Heavy damage in NEST splits the face and bursts one arm into a claw while the coat, the trousers and the other hand are still there. The claw has the reach. He is faster than the coated walk and still short of the full Super Tyrant. A grab with the claw impales. The human hand can still seize. Debris and a drop between levels can separate him from a target. They do not finish him.

## Super Tyrant

Pictures: `Super Tyrant claws.png` (remake) and `Super Tyrant original.png` (the older clawed heart-forward render).

The limiter is gone. Both arms are claws. The remake form carries a hood of flesh at the neck and a web of red vessels. The older render shows the chest cavity and the heart more openly. Either way the core in the chest is the place that staggers him.

- He lunges, sweeps and impales. A charge crosses a room. This is the form that runs.
- D's guns barely hurt it. The walkthrough's NEST note is the rule: small arms are a delay, not a kill.
- The chest core is the opening. Hits there break his rhythm long enough to move.
- The kill that destroys him, on D's side of the walkthrough, is a rocket launcher thrown down during the fight. Ada is the stranger on the catwalk. D does not see her throw it.
- On Claire's side he never reaches this form. William kills the coated Tyrant in the elevator first.

## Damnation

*Resident Evil: Damnation.* Same T-103 family as Mr. X. The Eastern Slav government keeps several of them under the presidential palace, coated and hatted, and sends them like soldiers. Svetlana Belikova gives the order. They step off the line and take the target she names. The film shows the obedience. It does not show a receiver. Chapter 3's implant is this story's account of that kind of command.

What the film adds past the remake stalker:

- More than one at a time. They wait, then move together when released.
- A spoken order is enough to point them. They will ignore a room full of other prey and take the named enemy.
- Open ground. Streets, a subway, a palace hall. The hunt is not limited to the RPD's corridors.
- Against Lickers. One coated Tyrant is dropped into a square of Plaga-driven Lickers. His punches burst them. He catches them out of the air and pulls them apart. Claws and tongues slice the coat and the hide underneath. He keeps killing until the numbers climb on him and drag him down. A single Licker is prey. A pack is the threat.
- The coat still fails under that damage. The body mutates in the palace fight: larger, the heart borne forward, the limiter gone. Same rule as the Super Tyrant. Small arms do not finish it.
- A human only matches him with a Dominant Plaga. Alexander "Buddy" Kozachenko takes the mutated Tyrant hand to hand, gets thrown through the room, gets back up, and forces the chest. Leon's shot into the exposed heart is the kill. Buddy's Plaga is the only thing in the film that lets a living person trade blows with one.

## This story, Chapter 3 only (RE2 Remake)

The body above is intact. These are extra conditions already on the page. They are not remake facts.

- He has been functionally lobotomized. A receiver delivers instructions. Orders are the main drive.
- A faint remainder of the person may still be under that. Pauses, a turned head and a refusal to take an easy hit can hint at it. They do not prove speech, a free choice or an alliance.
- A new signal can override the hesitation at any time. In the station he followed D, stopped, watched and did not commit when a crushing hit was open. He did not pursue at once when D left. A click came off him.
- A Mr. X Companion Token is only a proposed reward for the end of the game, and only after this original Tyrant is destroyed. It is not in hand.

## Detroit continuation — Ty (Scenes 75–89)

This is the **token-created companion**, not the original RE2 Tyrant that was destroyed. He is named **Ty**. His T-103 foundation already includes helicopter-throwing strength and exceptional durability. He voluntarily received small perfected T+G enhancements, developed healthy light-olive skin, bioelectricity and open-ended adaptation, and learned Chain Lightning, Water Walking and eleven other books in Scene 89.

**Receiver clarification (retroactive, first Ty transformation):** During the initial perfected T-virus transformation in Scene 78–79, **D physically removed Ty’s implanted command transmission receiver**. It is no longer present. Earlier passages describing an inactive but still implanted transmitter refer to the state before that removal and are superseded. Ty can hear and understand speech but does not speak; he communicates with gestures and, since Scene 89, basic sign language.
