# Loot Rules (Slayer Gamer Style: Symbiote Variant)

## Rarity Tiers
Poor > Common > Uncommon > Rare > Epic > Legendary > Mythic > God-tier

**Iconic** is an extra tag for items tied to a famous owner (e.g., Rebecca's shotgun).

## Every Kill Grants
**Biomass and DNA apply once the symbiote is bonded** (from D's first really good gacha pull). Before that, kills give XP and the tiered loot roll only.

1. **XP**
2. **Biomass:** raw organic matter the symbiote absorbs. It replaces the Soul Well. Biomass totals unlock symbiote powers (see the style bible, C13), and it also fuels morphs, regeneration and evolutions.
   - **After [Organic Metallization]** (from Doctor Doom's DNA): metal beings, robots, machines and raw metal count as Biomass.
   - **After the Swamp Thing upgrade:** plant matter counts at full value.
3. **DNA Sample:** progress toward sequencing that enemy's species (see DNA Sequencing)
4. **A loot roll** (see below)

## DNA Sequencing (Core Mechanic)
The symbiote copies the DNA of anything biological it fights.

- **A single touch captures the sample.** Sampling is never the bottleneck.
- **Each species has its own integration bar (0-100%)**, tracking how well the symbiote has adapted that DNA into usable traits.
- **Fighting the species and using its traits** advances integration.
- **A kill** adds a large chunk.
- **Consuming the body** adds more integration plus extra Biomass.
- **Sequence milestones:**
  - **25%:** a minor passive (resistance, sense or stat bump)
  - **50%:** a partial morph (one body part, such as claws, gills or wings)
  - **100%:** the species' signature trait (breath weapon, regeneration, venom) plus the option of a **full morph** into that form

**Tier scaling:** higher-tier enemies give bigger sequence chunks and rarer traits. A boss can hit 100% in one kill.

**Mixing:** two fully sequenced species can be **spliced** into a hybrid trait (wyvern wings + titan regeneration = regenerating flight muscle).

**Non-biological enemies** (robots, golems, undead, spirits) give **no DNA**. They still give normal loot, tech or magic parts, and a chance to unlock a skill. Once [Organic Metallization] is integrated, metal enemies also give Biomass.

**Trait examples:**
- Slime: Liquid Body, Absorption
- Licker: Wall-crawl, enhanced hearing
- Tyrant-class: Strength, durability (story limits still apply)
- Ivy / Plant 43-line: Growth, toxin, rooting

## Loot Roll (after the kill)

Roll rarity appropriate to the enemy’s tier (ordinary trash → Poor/Common heavy; elites/bosses → Rare+ possible). Then pick a concrete drop:

| Result flavor | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Cash / junk | Currency, scrap, comedy clutter |
| Ammo / consumable | Rounds, herbs, sprays, food |
| Weapon / gear | Guns, armor pieces, tools |
| Material | Crafting parts, samples, cores |
| Key / story | Keys, files, tokens — only if the scene supports it |

**Do not** auto-open every kill as a system loot box unless that campaign rule is active. RE2 challenge boxes and Tribute are tracked separately in Status/Inventory.
