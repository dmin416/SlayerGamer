# Wild Shapes and Druid Magic

Spells are grouped by what they do. Each tier measures how much of that power he can wield. Spell names in parentheses show the spells in each tier. Spells marked (D&D) come from D&D 5e. Spells marked (New) are original to this list. All others come from Warcraft.

## Tiers
- **Tier 1, Novice:** Basic bonds with nature.
- **Tier 2, Initiate:** Shaping the land and its creatures.
- **Tier 3, Adept:** Commanding the elements.
- **Tier 4, Master:** Reshaping the world itself.
- **Tier 5, Archdruid:** Power over life, death and the land on a grand scale.
- **Tier 6, Primordial:** Power rivaling the demigods of nature.

---

## Wild Shape
Taking the forms of beasts and nature.
- **Tier 1:** Small, swift forms for stealth, speed and swimming. *(Cat Form, Travel Form, Aquatic Form)*
  - **Energy:** ~50 to 80 MJ (12 to 20 kg TNT). Reshaping an 80 kg body into a similar-sized beast at ~1 MJ per kg reshaped.
- **Tier 2:** Powerful forms for fighting, flying and carrying an ally. *(Bear Form, Flight Form, Stag Form)*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 2 GJ (240 to 480 kg TNT). Building ~300 kg of extra bear tissue at ~5 MJ per kg plus reshaping the rest.
- **Tier 3:** Greater versions of each form, spellcasting forms and the shape of a living tree. Can force a beast shape onto another creature. *(Dire Bear Form, Swift Flight Form, Moonkin Form, Mount Form, Treant Form, Polymorph (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~5 to 25 GJ (1.2 to 6 t TNT). Forms of 1 to 5 tonnes such as a dire bear or treant.
- **Tier 4:** Ancient forms of great power. *(Tree of Life Form, Storm Crow Form)*
  - **Energy:** ~25 to 50 GJ (6 to 12 t TNT). Ancient forms of 5 to 10 tonnes.
- **Tier 5:** Reshapes whole groups into beasts at once and blends the traits of several beasts into one form. *(Animal Shapes (D&D), Hybrid Form (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~20 to 100 GJ (5 to 24 t TNT). Ten or more creatures reshaped at 2 to 10 GJ each.
  - **Balance Note:** Overlaps Tier 4 in total energy. Tier 5 spreads it across many bodies at once. Tier 4 packs it into one body and holds the form far longer.
- **Tier 6:** Takes the form of legendary beasts and towering ancients of immense size and power. *(Elder Beast Form (New), Ancient's Form (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.6 to 6 TJ (140 t to 1.4 kt TNT). Legendary forms of 100 to 1,000 tonnes at ~6 MJ per kg.

## Feral Combat
Fighting with claw, fang and hide while in beast form.
- **Tier 1:** Clawing, biting, swiping, stalking unseen and drawing an enemy's attention. *(Shred, Rake, Swipe, Mangle, Maul, Growl, Prowl)*
  - **Energy:** ~1 kJ per strike (0.24 g TNT). Comparable to a big cat's swipe.
- **Tier 2:** Bleeding wounds, finishing bites, charging leaps, bursts of speed, hardened fur and interrupting spellcasters. *(Thrash, Rip, Ferocious Bite, Dash, Wild Charge, Ironfur, Skull Bash)*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 50 kJ (2 to 12 g TNT). A 300 kg bear charging at 15 m/s carries ~34 kJ.
- **Tier 3:** Stunning blows, surges of ferocity, rapid regeneration and roars that stun foes or rally allies. *(Maim, Tiger's Fury, Frenzied Regeneration, Incapacitating Roar, Stampeding Roar)*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 MJ (0.24 to 2.4 kg TNT). Mostly the rapid regeneration of a few kilograms of tissue.
- **Tier 4:** Berserk frenzies, tearing many foes at once and enduring near-fatal harm. *(Berserk, Primal Wrath, Survival Instincts)*
  - **Energy:** ~5 to 20 MJ (1.2 to 5 kg TNT). A 600 kg beast fighting at ~50 kW for a few minutes.
- **Tier 5:** Becomes the avatar of the Great Bear Spirit and howls that drive allies into a feral frenzy. *(Incarnation: Guardian of the Great Bear, Primal Howl (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 GJ (24 kg TNT). A multi-tonne spirit form fighting at ~1 MW for a minute or two.
- **Tier 6:** Channels the spirits of every great beast at once, gaining their combined fury and resilience, and calls a pack of beast spirits to fight beside him. *(Avatar of the Wild (New), Spirit Pack (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 GJ (240 kg TNT). A dozen great spirit beasts fighting at ~1 MW each for minutes.
  - **Balance Note:** Low next to other Tier 6 powers. Feral power is raw force in short, violent bursts lasting seconds to minutes. It reshapes nothing lasting.

## Plant Manipulation
Commanding roots, vines, thorns and growth.
- **Tier 1:** Small nature effects, empowering wooden weapons, growing thorns, binding a foe in roots, setting snares and hurling bolts of nature energy. *(Druidcraft (D&D), Shillelagh (D&D), Thorns, Entangling Roots, Snare (D&D), Wrath)*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 20 MJ (0.24 to 5 kg TNT). Growing up to ~5 kg of fresh plant matter at ~4 MJ per kg.
- **Tier 2:** Bark-hard skin, spiked ground, binding groups in roots, speeding growth across an area and speaking with plants. *(Barkskin, Mass Entanglement, Spike Growth (D&D), Plant Growth (D&D), Speak with Plants (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.2 to 25 GJ (50 kg to 6 t TNT). Spiked ground over ~120 m² up to overgrowth across ~3,000 m².
  - **Balance Note:** Can exceed Tier 3. Tier 2 growth is slow, takes hours and draws most of its energy from sunlight and soil. Tier 3 is instant and concentrated, and awakening grants a mind, which costs little physical energy.
- **Tier 3:** Awakens plants and beasts to thought and speech and grows vines that crush foes and drag them underground. *(Awaken (D&D), Strangling Vines (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 GJ (240 kg TNT). Growing a few hundred kilograms of crushing vines or awakening a tree.
- **Tier 4:** Grows great walls of thorns and wards an entire grove. *(Wall of Thorns (D&D), Druid Grove (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~30 to 100 GJ (7 to 24 t TNT). A wall of thorns holds ~8 tonnes of brush at ~4 MJ per kg.
- **Tier 5:** Raises entire forests in moments and fills them with thorns that turn on invaders. *(Forestbirth (New), Thornwild (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~50 to 500 TJ (12 to 120 kt TNT). A mature forest of 10 to 100 hectares at ~17 MJ per kg of dry wood.
- **Tier 6:** Grows a world tree and spreads roots that bind a whole land together. *(World Tree Seed (New), Rootbind the Land (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e17 J (~24 Mt TNT). A world tree of ~10 billion kg of dry wood.
  - **Balance Note:** Far beyond most Tier 6 powers in total. A world tree grows over years and draws on sunlight and the land the whole time. The druid supplies the seed of the effect, not all of the energy at once.

## Earth and Stone Manipulation
Shaping soil, rock and the land itself.
- **Tier 1:** Moving small amounts of earth, empowering stones and shaking the ground. *(Mold Earth (D&D), Magic Stone (D&D), Earth Tremor (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 MJ (24 g TNT). Lifting ~5 tonnes of soil about 1.5 m.
- **Tier 2:** Stepping into stone, erupting the ground and pulling flying foes down. *(Meld into Stone (D&D), Erupting Earth (D&D), Earthbind (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 MJ (0.24 kg TNT). Throwing ~30 tonnes of ground upward at ~6 m/s.
  - **Balance Note:** Small next to Tier 3. Tier 2 moves loose ground briefly. Tier 3 breaks the bonds of solid rock, which costs far more.
- **Tier 3:** Sculpting stone, raising walls of rock, turning rock to mud and disguising the land. *(Stone Shape (D&D), Wall of Stone (D&D), Transmute Rock (D&D), Hallucinatory Terrain (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 GJ (240 kg to 2.4 t TNT). Breaking ~1,000 tonnes of rock into mud at ~5 MJ per tonne.
- **Tier 4:** Moving hills, raising stone pillars, turning flesh to stone, filling the body with earth and remaking how a whole region looks. *(Move Earth (D&D), Bones of the Earth (D&D), Flesh to Stone (D&D), Investiture of Stone (D&D), Mirage Arcane (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~35 GJ (~8 t TNT). Moving a small hill of ~360,000 tonnes about 10 m.
- **Tier 5:** Splitting the earth with violent quakes and tearing chasms across the land. *(Earthquake (D&D), Rift (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~2 to 60 TJ (0.5 to 15 kt TNT). An earthquake of magnitude 5 to 6.
- **Tier 6:** Raises or sinks mountains and reshapes coastlines. *(Mountain's Rise (New), Shatter Coast (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e16 J (~2.4 Mt TNT). Raising 1 km³ of granite an average of 500 m.

## Fire Manipulation
Calling and commanding flame.
- **Tier 1:** Producing, shaping and spreading small fires. *(Produce Flame (D&D), Control Flames (D&D), Create Bonfire (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 kJ to 1 MJ (2 g to 0.24 kg TNT). Bolts of flame up to a small bonfire.
- **Tier 2:** Blades and spheres of fire, superheating metal, everburning flames and flaming arrows. *(Flame Blade (D&D), Flaming Sphere (D&D), Heat Metal (D&D), Continual Flame (D&D), Flame Arrows (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 MJ (0.24 to 2.4 kg TNT). Heating 5 kg of steel by 500 °C takes ~1.25 MJ.
- **Tier 3:** Walls of blazing fire and controlled burns that clear the land without harming chosen plants. *(Wall of Fire (D&D), Cleansing Burn (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 2 GJ (240 to 480 kg TNT). An 18 m wall of fire burning at ~2 MW per meter for a minute.
  - **Balance Note:** Can exceed some Tier 4 powers in other categories. Fire releases the energy stored in its fuel. The druid directs it and does not create it.
- **Tier 4:** Filling the body with fire and calling firestorms over wide areas. *(Investiture of Flame (D&D), Fire Storm (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 GJ (2.4 to 24 t TNT). A ~1,000 m² firestorm at ~100 MW for several minutes.
- **Tier 5:** Drifting clouds of burning embers and rains of fire. *(Incendiary Cloud (D&D), Ember Rain (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 TJ (240 t to 2.4 kt TNT). Burning the fuel of 1 to 10 hectares of forest.
- **Tier 6:** Calls forth volcanic fire from the depths of the earth and rivers of magma. *(Volcanic Rise (New), Magma Flow (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e15 to 1e17 J (240 kt to 24 Mt TNT). A small volcanic eruption.

## Water and Ice Manipulation
Commanding water, frost and fog.
- **Tier 1:** Shaping, creating or removing small amounts of water, numbing frost, ice shards and fog. *(Shape Water (D&D), Create or Destroy Water (D&D), Frostbite (D&D), Ice Knife (D&D), Fog Cloud (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 1 MJ (24 g to 0.24 kg TNT). Freezing 1 kg of water takes ~0.33 MJ.
- **Tier 2:** Crashing waves, walls of water, sleet storms, breathing underwater and walking on water. *(Tidal Wave (D&D), Wall of Water (D&D), Sleet Storm (D&D), Water Breathing (D&D), Water Walk (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 5 MJ (0.24 to 1.2 kg TNT). A wave of ~80 tonnes of water lifted and thrown.
- **Tier 3:** Parting or flooding bodies of water, trapping foes in water, hailstorms and blasts of freezing air. *(Control Water (D&D), Watery Sphere (D&D), Ice Storm (D&D), Cone of Cold (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 5 GJ (240 kg to 1.2 t TNT). Parting ~28,000 tonnes of water about 15 m.
- **Tier 4:** Filling the body with frost and encasing an area in ice. *(Investiture of Ice (D&D), Glacial Prison (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 1 TJ (24 to 240 t TNT). Freezing up to ~2,700 m³ of water into ice.
- **Tier 5:** Towering walls of water that crash over the land and blizzards that bury a region. *(Tsunami (D&D), Whiteout (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 100 TJ (24 t to 24 kt TNT). A 30 m tsunami wall up to a blizzard burying 10 km² in snow.
  - **Balance Note:** A very wide range. A tsunami is one violent strike lasting seconds. A blizzard is weaker per second but lasts hours across a whole region.
- **Tier 6:** Commands the tides and freezes or thaws entire seas. *(Command the Tides (New), Frozen Sea (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e16 to 1e18 J (2.4 to 240 Mt TNT). Freezing 1,000 km² of sea 1 m deep takes ~3e17 J.

## Wind Manipulation
Commanding air, storms and lightning.
- **Tier 1:** Bursts of thunder around himself and gentle breezes that carry scents, cool the air or snuff out small flames. *(Thunderclap (D&D), Breeze (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 kJ (0.24 to 2.4 g TNT). A thunderclap or a light breeze.
- **Tier 2:** Knockback blasts, small whirlwinds, deflecting winds, walls of wind, lifting a foe in a cyclone and writing in the clouds. *(Typhoon, Cyclone, Dust Devil (D&D), Warding Wind (D&D), Wind Wall (D&D), Skywrite (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 100 MJ (0.24 to 24 kg TNT). A wall of 30 m/s wind sustained for a minute runs at ~1 MW.
- **Tier 3:** Hurricanes of wind and lightning and command of the winds across an area. *(Hurricane, Control Winds (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~5 to 20 GJ (1.2 to 5 t TNT). A local hurricane at ~90 MW for a minute plus lightning bolts of ~1 to 5 GJ each.
- **Tier 4:** Filling the body with wind for flight and protection and gales that carry a whole group through the sky. *(Investiture of Wind (D&D), Skyward Gale (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 40 GJ (2.4 to 10 t TNT). Carrying a group through the sky at ~10 MW for an hour.
- **Tier 5:** Command of the weather over vast lands and storms of vengeance. *(Control Weather (D&D), Storm of Vengeance (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e14 to 1e15 J (24 to 240 kt TNT). A single thunderstorm releases ~1e15 J.
- **Tier 6:** Shifts the seasons and climate of an entire region and raises storms that never end. *(Turn of Seasons (New), Eternal Storm (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e18 to 1e20 J (240 Mt to 24 Gt TNT). Warming the lower air over 10,000 km² by 10 °C takes ~1e20 J.
  - **Balance Note:** Far beyond most Tier 6 powers in total. The change lasts a whole season, so the energy spreads over months at an average of ~1e13 W.

## Sun and Moon
Drawing on sunlight, moonlight and the stars.
- **Tier 1:** Burning foes with lunar and solar light and outlining them in glowing light. *(Moonfire, Sunfire, Faerie Fire (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 50 kJ (2 to 12 g TNT). A burning bolt of light.
- **Tier 2:** Beams of moonlight, bursts of celestial energy, filling an area with daylight and silencing foes with sunbeams. *(Starfire, Starsurge, Daylight (D&D), Solar Beam)*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 50 MJ (2.4 to 12 kg TNT). Daylight over a wide area for an hour at ~10 kW.
- **Tier 3:** Raining stars, shifting between solar and lunar power and empowering moonlight casting. *(Starfall, Eclipse, Warrior of the Moon)*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 1 GJ (24 to 240 kg TNT). Dozens of falling stars at ~1 to 10 MJ each.
- **Tier 4:** Calling down the moon's full power and uniting sun and moon. *(Fury of the Moon, Celestial Alignment)*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 GJ (240 kg to 2.4 t TNT). A beam of moonlight at ~100 MW to 1 GW for ~10 seconds.
- **Tier 5:** Explosions of blinding sunlight and moonlight that crashes down over a wide area. *(Sunburst (D&D), Moonfall (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 1 TJ (24 to 240 t TNT). A blinding burst of sunlight over a wide area.
- **Tier 6:** Turns day to night or night to day over a region. *(Eternal Night (New), Dawnbringer (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e17 to 5e17 J (24 to 120 Mt TNT). Sunlight of ~1 kW per m² over 10,000 km² for 12 hours.
  - **Balance Note:** The energy spreads over 12 hours of sustained light or darkness, not a single burst.

## Beast Communion
Speaking with, bonding with and commanding beasts and insects.
- **Tier 1:** Speaking with beasts, bonding with one, calming them and putting them to sleep. *(Speak with Animals (D&D), Beast Bond (D&D), Soothe, Hibernate)*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 1 MJ (24 g to 0.24 kg TNT). Holding a link to one beast at ~100 W for an hour.
- **Tier 2:** Sending beasts as messengers and seeing through their senses. *(Animal Messenger (D&D), Beast Sense (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 MJ (0.24 to 2.4 kg TNT). Holding a link for a full day.
- **Tier 3:** Dominating a beast's will, growing giant insects and calling swarms. *(Dominate Beast (D&D), Giant Insect (D&D), Insect Plague (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 5 GJ (240 kg to 1.2 t TNT). Mostly growing ~1 tonne of giant insects.
- **Tier 4:** Commands every beast within a forest and sees through many of them at once. *(Lord of the Forest (New), Shared Senses (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 GJ (240 kg to 2.4 t TNT). Linking ~10,000 beasts at ~100 W each for an hour.
  - **Balance Note:** Can match or fall below Tier 3. Tier 3 grows real insect bodies that last. Tier 4 is only mental links, cheap in energy and wide in reach.
- **Tier 5:** Calls the beasts of an entire land to war and moves great herds across the land. *(Call of the Wild (New), Great Migration (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 TJ (2.4 to 24 kt TNT). Linking millions of beasts for about a day.
- **Tier 6:** Speaks with and is heeded by the great beast spirits and bonds with one of them. *(Voice of the Spirits (New), Spirit Bond (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e15 J (~240 kt TNT). Linking with demigod-scale beast spirits. Rough assumption.
  - **Balance Note:** No physical measure exists for bonding with a great spirit. This figure is a placeholder.

## Poison and Decay
Withering and sickening.
- **Tier 1:** Noxious gas and parasites. *(Poison Spray (D&D), Infestation (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 kJ (0.24 to 2.4 g TNT). A puff of toxin.
- **Tier 2:** Venomous thorns and spores that sap strength over time. *(Venom Thorn (New), Sapping Spores (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 kJ (2 to 24 g TNT). Seeding thorns and spores.
- **Tier 3:** Draining life from creatures and plants and inflicting disease. *(Blight (D&D), Contagion (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 GJ (24 kg TNT). Drawing ~50 kg of water out of a body takes ~0.11 GJ.
- **Tier 4:** Spreading blight across fields and forests and rotting wood, food and flesh. *(Creeping Blight (New), Rot (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 30 TJ (240 t to 7 kt TNT). Rotting up to ~2,000 tonnes of forest across ~10 hectares.
  - **Balance Note:** A large jump from Tier 3. Tier 4 mostly speeds up natural rot over days. Most of that energy comes from the plants' own stored chemical energy, not the druid.
- **Tier 5:** Plagues and swarms that sweep through whole populations. *(Pestilence (New), Plague Swarm (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e14 to 1e15 J (24 to 240 kt TNT). Plague and rot across a region.
- **Tier 6:** Withers an entire land to dust and leaves it barren for generations. *(Withering (New), Dead Land (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e18 J (~240 Mt TNT). Withering the plant life of ~10,000 km².
  - **Balance Note:** Most of this is the stored energy of dead plant life released as it rots over months.

## Cleansing
Purging poison, curses and corruption.
- **Tier 1:** Sensing poison and disease, purifying food and drink and cleansing curses and poison. *(Detect Poison and Disease (D&D), Purify Food and Drink (D&D), Remove Corruption)*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 kJ (0.24 to 2.4 g TNT). Matching the small poisons and curses it removes.
- **Tier 2:** Cleansing harmful magic and washing away charms and illusions. *(Nature's Cure, Spirit Wash (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 kJ (2 to 24 g TNT). Undoing charms and illusions.
- **Tier 3:** Purging deep curses and lasting afflictions. *(Uncurse (New), Mend Affliction (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 1 GJ (24 to 240 kg TNT). Matching the strength of a deep curse or blight.
- **Tier 4:** Cleansing corruption from an entire grove or village with purifying rain. *(Cleansing Rain (New), Grove Purification (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 TJ (~240 t TNT). Condensing ~1,000 tonnes of purifying rain over a village.
  - **Balance Note:** A large jump from Tier 3. Most of the cost is forming rain clouds over a village, which nature finishes once the druid starts it.
- **Tier 5:** Purifying blighted lands and poisoned waters across a region. *(Purify the Land (New), Living Spring (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 TJ (2.4 to 24 kt TNT). Purifying the waters and soil of a region.
- **Tier 6:** Purging demonic or otherworldly corruption from the land itself. *(Banish Corruption (New), Sanctify the Wild (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e17 to 1e18 J (24 to 240 Mt TNT). Matching corruption on the scale of a whole land.

## Healing
Restoring life.
- **Tier 1:** Healing over time, quick heals, strong direct heals and healing berries. *(Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Healing Touch, Goodberry (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 MJ (0.24 kg TNT). Rebuilding ~0.2 kg of tissue at ~5 MJ per kg.
- **Tier 2:** Healing groups, healing flowers on the ground, blooming heals, instant heals and returning the fallen to life outside battle. *(Wild Growth, Efflorescence, Lifebloom, Swiftmend, Revive)*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 50 MJ (2.4 to 12 kg TNT). Healing a small group or repairing a dead body's damaged organs.
- **Tier 3:** Returning the fallen in battle, shielding allies in bark, instant spellcasting and restoring an ally's mana. *(Rebirth, Ironbark, Nature's Swiftness, Innervate)*
  - **Energy:** ~50 to 100 MJ (12 to 24 kg TNT). Raising the fallen in battle and shielding allies.
- **Tier 4:** Waves of healing over everyone nearby, becoming a living tree of healing and feasts that heal and strengthen. *(Tranquility, Incarnation: Tree of Life, Heroes' Feast (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.5 GJ (~120 kg TNT). Rebuilding ~80 kg of tissue across dozens of wounded.
- **Tier 5:** Healing an entire army at once and filling a battlefield with healing growth. *(Mass Renewal (New), Field of Life (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~50 GJ (~12 t TNT). Rebuilding ~1 kg of tissue for each of 10,000 soldiers.
- **Tier 6:** Restoring the dead long after death and regrowing their bodies from the roots. *(Second Spring (New), Return from the Roots (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.5 GJ per body (~120 kg TNT). Regrowing a full body. Calling a soul back has no physical measure.
  - **Balance Note:** Per body this is lower than Tier 5 in total. Tier 5 spreads a little healing across thousands. Tier 6 rebuilds one whole body from nothing, about 100 times the energy per person, and calls back a soul, which has no physical cost to measure.

## Warding
Protecting and empowering allies.
- **Tier 1:** Small protections, absorbing elemental harm, warding off otherworldly creatures and blessing allies with nature's strength. *(Resistance (D&D), Absorb Elements (D&D), Protection from Evil and Good (D&D), Mark of the Wild)*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 kJ (0.24 to 2.4 g TNT). Absorbing a sword blow or a bolt of flame.
- **Tier 2:** Imbuing weapons with elemental power and setting stones that ward a small area. *(Elemental Weapon (D&D), Wardstone (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 kJ (2 to 24 g TNT). Empowering weapon strikes and warding a small area.
- **Tier 3:** Freeing allies from restraint and barriers that keep living things out. *(Freedom of Movement (D&D), Antilife Shell (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 MJ (0.24 to 2.4 kg TNT). Holding back creatures pushing against a barrier.
  - **Balance Note:** Small next to other Tier 3 powers. Wards cost almost nothing while idle and spend energy only when struck.
- **Tier 4:** Warding a whole camp or village with rings of thorns and protective circles. *(Thorn Ward (New), Circle of Protection (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 GJ (~240 kg TNT). Absorbing siege stones of ~1 MJ each and fire.
- **Tier 5:** Shielding an entire city or forest from attack. *(Living Barrier (New), Sanctuary of the Wild (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 10 TJ (24 t to 2.4 kt TNT). Holding a city's or forest's shield against bombardment.
- **Tier 6:** Sealing a land against invasion from other worlds. *(World Seal (New), Planar Ward (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1e15 to 1e17 J (240 kt to 24 Mt TNT). Holding back a breach from another world.

## Mind and Force
Bending minds and pulling or reversing force.
- **Tier 1:** Charming a person and calming them with soothing scents. *(Charm Person (D&D), Calming Scent (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 kJ (0.24 g TNT). Influencing a single mind.
- **Tier 2:** Vortexes that pull enemies inward and suggestions whispered through dreams. *(Ursol's Vortex, Dream Whisper (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 kJ (2.4 g TNT). Dragging five people about 5 m.
- **Tier 3:** Confusing minds, charming any creature and binding them to commands. *(Confusion (D&D), Charm Monster (D&D), Geas (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 kJ (2 to 24 g TNT). Influencing several minds at once.
- **Tier 4:** Reversing gravity and inscribing harmful glyphs. *(Reverse Gravity (D&D), Symbol (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 MJ (0.24 to 2.4 kg TNT). Lifting creatures and up to ~10 tonnes of debris about 30 m.
- **Tier 5:** Shattering intellects and making places repel or attract creatures. *(Feeblemind (D&D), Antipathy/Sympathy (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 MJ (2.4 to 24 kg TNT). Holding an attraction or repulsion over an area for days.
- **Tier 6:** Swaying the will of crowds and armies. *(Voice of the Wild (New), Mass Sway (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 GJ (240 kg to 2.4 t TNT). Influencing ~10,000 minds at ~100 W each for an hour.
  - **Balance Note:** Tiny next to other Tier 6 powers. Influencing minds moves almost no physical energy. Its strength lies in how many minds it sways and for how long, often days or more.

## Nature's Sight
Sensing, finding and foreseeing.
- **Tier 1:** Small guidance and sensing magic. *(Guidance (D&D), Detect Magic (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 kJ (0.24 g TNT). Brief sensing.
- **Tier 2:** Omens, seeing in darkness, sensing traps and finding beasts, plants and objects. *(Augury (D&D), Darkvision (D&D), Find Traps (D&D), Locate Animals or Plants (D&D), Locate Object (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 kJ (2 to 24 g TNT). Searching an area or seeing in darkness for hours.
- **Tier 3:** Finding creatures, questioning the spirits of nature, knowing the surrounding land and seeing from afar. *(Locate Creature (D&D), Divination (D&D), Commune with Nature (D&D), Scrying (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 MJ (0.24 kg TNT). Seeing and hearing from far away.
- **Tier 4:** Knowing the path to any place and seeing through the trees of a forest. *(Find the Path (D&D), Eyes of the Forest (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 MJ (2.4 kg TNT). Sight through every tree of a forest.
- **Tier 5:** Glimpsing the immediate future and receiving prophecies through dreams. *(Foresight (D&D), Dream Prophecy (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~100 MJ (24 kg TNT). Visions of the future. Rough assumption.
  - **Balance Note:** Small next to other Tier 5 powers. Sensing and foresight gather information, not force, and last hours to days.
- **Tier 6:** Seeing through every plant and creature across a land at once. *(Worldsight (New), Root Network (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 TJ (~240 t TNT). Sensing through ~1 trillion organisms at ~1 mW each for an hour.
  - **Balance Note:** Small next to other Tier 6 powers. The cost is holding countless points of sight at once, not producing force.

## Summoning
Calling beasts, fey, treants and elementals.
- **Tier 1:** Calling a wisp or small nature spirit to aid him. *(Call Wisp (New), Spirit Mote (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 MJ (0.24 to 2.4 kg TNT). Forming a spirit about 1 kg in substance.
- **Tier 2:** Calling beast and fey spirits. *(Conjure Animals (D&D), Summon Beast (D&D), Summon Fey (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.5 to 2 GJ (120 to 480 kg TNT). Forming ~300 kg of beasts at ~6 MJ per kg.
- **Tier 3:** Calling treants, woodland fey and elementals, binding otherworldly creatures and weakening foes to an element. *(Force of Nature, Conjure Woodland Beings (D&D), Conjure Minor Elementals (D&D), Summon Elemental (D&D), Conjure Elemental (D&D), Planar Binding (D&D), Elemental Bane (D&D))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 40 GJ (2.4 to 10 t TNT). Forming treants or elementals of 2 to 10 tonnes.
- **Tier 4:** Calling powerful fey and elder elementals. *(Conjure Fey (D&D), Call Elder Elemental (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 0.3 TJ (24 to 72 t TNT). Forming an elder elemental of ~50 tonnes.
- **Tier 5:** Calling the ancients of the forest and its guardians to battle. *(Call the Ancients (New), Grove Guardians (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1.5 TJ (~360 t TNT). Forming several forest ancients of ~50 tonnes each.
- **Tier 6:** Calling the great beast spirits themselves and a host of primal spirits. *(Summon Great Spirit (New), Primal Host (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 TJ (2.4 to 24 kt TNT). Calling great spirits with forms of ~1,000 tonnes or more.

## Travel and Utility
Moving through the world and reshaping small things.
- **Tier 1:** Mending small breaks and walking over brush and snow without hindrance. *(Mending (D&D), Leafwalk (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 10 kJ (0.24 to 2.4 g TNT). Fusing a small break or easing each step.
- **Tier 2:** Growing or shrinking creatures and objects and returning to the Moonglade. *(Enlarge/Reduce (D&D), Teleport: Moonglade)*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 5 MJ (0.24 to 1.2 kg TNT). A fixed, attuned destination like the Moonglade cuts the cost of a 1,000 km jump to about a hundredth.
  - **Balance Note:** Can cost less than Tier 3. The Moonglade jump is cheap only because the destination is fixed and attuned. Tier 3 works from any tree without an anchor.
- **Tier 3:** Stepping from one tree to another and traveling underground through roots. *(Tree Stride (D&D), Root Road (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.06 to 40 MJ (15 g to 10 kg TNT). Tree-to-tree steps up to root roads of ~100 km.
- **Tier 4:** Walking the Emerald Dream and planting seeds that become return points. *(Dreamwalk, Seed Gate (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~1 to 4 GJ (240 kg to 1 t TNT). Crossing ~10,000 km at about the energy of a ballistic launch over that distance.
- **Tier 5:** Bringing others through the Emerald Dream and opening gates between groves. *(Dream Caravan (New), Grove Gate (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~10 to 100 GJ (2.4 to 24 t TNT). Moving a group of ~20 people across a world.
- **Tier 6:** Opening paths between worlds through nature. *(Worldroot Path (New), Rift of the Wild (New))*
  - **Energy:** ~0.1 to 10 TJ (24 t to 2.4 kt TNT). Escaping a world takes ~5 GJ per person. Opening a lasting path costs far more.
