# DC

## DC Heroes

### Superman
- **Locations:** Metropolis, the Daily Planet, Smallville, the Fortress of Solitude, Krypton
- **Powers:** Flight, super strength, super speed, invulnerability, heat vision, freeze breath, X-ray vision, super hearing
- **Items:** Kryptonian crystals, the Kryptonian ship, the Fortress's Kryptonian AI
- **Weapons:** None

### Batman
- **Locations:** Gotham City, the Batcave, Wayne Manor, Arkham Asylum
- **Powers:** None (peak human, detective)
- **Items:** Batmobile, Batwing, Batsuits, utility belt, Bat-Signal
- **Weapons:** Batarangs, grapnel gun, Kryptonite spear, Hellbat armor

### Wonder Woman
- **Locations:** Themyscira, the WWI Western Front, Washington, D.C.
- **Powers:** Amazon strength, flight, divine heritage
- **Items:** Lasso of Truth, tiara, Golden Eagle armor
- **Weapons:** Godkiller sword, shield, Bracelets of Submission

### The Flash
- **Locations:** Central City, S.T.A.R. Labs, the Speed Force
- **Powers:** The Speed Force, time travel, phasing, lightning throwing
- **Items:** Flash ring suit, Cosmic Treadmill
- **Weapons:** Lightning throws

### Aquaman
- **Locations:** Atlantis, the Trench, the Hidden Sea, Xebel
- **Powers:** Atlantean strength, sea-life telepathy
- **Items:** Atlantean armor
- **Weapons:** The Trident of Atlan

### Green Lantern Corps
- **Locations:** Oa, Coast City, the Central Power Battery
- **Powers:** Willpower energy constructs
- **Items:** Green Lantern power ring, power battery
- **Weapons:** Ring constructs

### Cyborg
- **Locations:** S.T.A.R. Labs, Detroit
- **Powers:** Technopathy
- **Items:** Mother Box body
- **Weapons:** Sonic cannon

### Shazam
- **Locations:** Philadelphia, the Rock of Eternity
- **Powers:** The wisdom of Solomon, strength of Hercules, stamina of Atlas, power of Zeus, courage of Achilles, speed of Mercury
- **Items:** The Staff of the Wizard
- **Weapons:** Lightning

### Martian Manhunter
- **Locations:** Mars
- **Powers:** Shapeshifting, telepathy, phasing, invisibility, flight
- **Items:** None
- **Weapons:** None

### Supergirl
- **Locations:** Argo City, the Fortress of Solitude
- **Powers:** Kryptonian powers
- **Items:** Krypto the Superdog
- **Weapons:** None

### Green Arrow
- **Locations:** Star City
- **Powers:** None
- **Items:** None
- **Weapons:** Trick arrows (boxing glove, net, explosive)

### Doctor Fate
- **Locations:** The Tower of Fate
- **Powers:** Lord of Order magic
- **Items:** The Helmet of Fate, the Amulet of Anubis
- **Weapons:** Ankh constructs

### John Constantine and Zatanna
- **Locations:** London, the House of Mystery, Hell
- **Powers:** Occult magic, backwards-spoken spells (Zatanna)
- **Items:** Holy relics, the Spear of Destiny
- **Weapons:** Spells

### Blue Beetle
- **Locations:** Palmera City, Kord Industries
- **Powers:** Scarab armor
- **Items:** The Scarab (Khaji-Da)
- **Weapons:** Scarab armor weapon constructs

### Hawkman and Hawkgirl
- **Locations:** Thanagar
- **Powers:** Reincarnation, flight
- **Items:** Nth metal wings
- **Weapons:** Nth metal mace
