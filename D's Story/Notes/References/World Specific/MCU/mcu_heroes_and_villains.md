# The MCU: The Nature of the Universe, Its Heroes and Its Villains

## Why the MCU Never Stops Fighting

Gotham is one sick city. The MCU is a whole universe (and then a multiverse) that keeps getting louder. The fighting never ends for different reasons.

- **Earth is a beacon.** Earth holds or attracts the Infinity Stones, the Tesseract and later multiversal attention. Every time humanity shows power, something bigger notices. Loki and the Chitauri came for the Tesseract. Thanos came for the Stones. Galactus and the multiverse came after that.
- **Escalation through heroes.** Gotham escalates because of Batman. The MCU escalates because of the Avengers. Tony Stark builds Ultron out of fear after New York. Hydra hides inside S.H.I.E.L.D. for decades. Wanda's grief creates Westview. Heroes cause many of the threats they later fight.
- **Legacy and grudges.** Many villains are personal. Obadiah Stane, Ivan Vanko, Aldrich Killian, Vulture and Mysterio all trace back to Tony Stark. Zemo traces back to Sokovia. Killmonger traces back to Wakanda's own sins. Kingpin and Daredevil keep circling each other.
- **Power vacuums.** The Blip erased half of all life for five years. When everyone came back, borders, jobs, homes and governments were broken. The Flag-Smashers, the Thunderbolts program and Valentina's schemes all grew in that chaos.
- **Governments against heroes.** The Sokovia Accords split the Avengers in *Civil War*. Ross, Valentina and the CIA keep trying to control or replace heroes, which creates new enemies inside the system.
- **The multiverse breaks open.** Loki freeing the timelines from He Who Remains, Doctor Strange's spells and Wanda's hunt for her children cracked reality. Incursions between universes are now the biggest threat in the setting and the driver behind *Avengers: Doomsday* (Dec. 18, 2026) and *Avengers: Secret Wars* (Dec. 17, 2027).
- **Myth over realism.** Batman defends one city forever. The Avengers keep saving the world, and every save sets up a bigger threat. New York leads to Ultron, Ultron leads to the Accords, the Accords leave Earth divided when Thanos arrives and the Snap leads to the multiverse.

## The Heroes

### The Original Avengers

**Iron Man (Tony Stark)**
Genius billionaire and weapons maker who built his first suit in a cave after being captured by the Ten Rings. Started the MCU and the Avengers' greatest successes and mistakes. Created Ultron. Led the pro-Accords side in *Civil War*. Died using the Stark Infinity Gauntlet to destroy Thanos and his army in *Endgame*. His legacy shapes Spider-Man, Riri Williams, Harley Keener and nearly every tech villain in the setting.

**Captain America (Steve Rogers)**
Super soldier from WWII frozen in ice and revived in the modern day. The moral center of the Avengers. Refused the Accords. Lifted Mjolnir in *Endgame*, returned the Stones and stayed in the past to live with Peggy Carter. Passed the shield to Sam Wilson.

**Thor**
Asgardian god of thunder. Lost his mother, father, brother, home planet and half his people across the series. Wielded Mjolnir, then Stormbreaker. Now raising Love, the daughter of Gorr the God Butcher.

**Hulk (Bruce Banner)**
Scientist who turns into a rage-fueled giant. Lost control on Earth, became a gladiator on Sakaar and merged his two sides into Smart Hulk. Snapped everyone back in *Endgame* at the cost of his arm.

**Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)**
Former Red Room assassin turned S.H.I.E.L.D. agent. The glue that held the Avengers together after the Snap. Sacrificed herself on Vormir for the Soul Stone.

**Hawkeye (Clint Barton)**
Master archer and family man. Became the killer Ronin during the Blip. Trained Kate Bishop as his successor.

### The New Generation

**Captain America (Sam Wilson)**
Former Falcon who took up the shield. Fought the Flag-Smashers and Red Hulk. Rebuilding the Avengers.

**Bucky Barnes (Winter Soldier)**
Steve's best friend, brainwashed by Hydra into an assassin. Freed and recovered. Became a congressman and joined Yelena's team in *Thunderbolts\**.

**Spider-Man (Peter Parker)**
Teen hero mentored by Tony Stark. Lost Aunt May and his identity in *No Way Home* when Doctor Strange's spell made the whole world forget him. In *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* (July 31, 2026) he lives alone and works on his own while his powers start changing.

**Doctor Strange (Stephen Strange)**
Surgeon turned Sorcerer Supreme. Saw 14,000,605 futures and found the one where the Avengers win. His spells caused some of the multiverse damage.

**Black Panther (T'Challa, then Shuri)**
T'Challa led Wakanda into the world and died of illness offscreen. Shuri took the mantle and fought Namor's Talokan.

**Captain Marvel (Carol Danvers)**
Former pilot powered by the Space Stone's energy. One of the strongest heroes in the setting. Leads *The Marvels* with Monica Rambeau and Ms. Marvel.

**Scarlet Witch (Wanda Maximoff)**
Chaos magic user and one of the most powerful beings in the MCU. Killed Vision to stop Thanos, created Westview out of grief and became the villain of *Multiverse of Madness*. Apparently died bringing down Mount Wundagore.

**Vision**
Android born from Ultron's body and the Mind Stone. Killed by Thanos. A white Vision was rebuilt and is getting his own story in *VisionQuest*.

**Ant-Man (Scott Lang) and the Wasp (Hope van Dyne)**
Shrinking heroes who discovered the Quantum Realm path that made the Time Heist possible. Fought Kang in the Quantum Realm.

**Guardians of the Galaxy**
Star-Lord, Gamora, Drax, Rocket, Groot, Mantis and Nebula. Space misfits who became a family. Disbanded after *Vol. 3* and Rocket now leads a new team.

**Shang-Chi**
Martial artist and son of Wenwu. Took control of the Ten Rings after his father's death.

**Loki**
Thor's brother and the first Avengers villain. Became an antihero, then a god. Now sits at the center of the multiverse holding the timelines together.

**Daredevil (Matt Murdock)**
Blind lawyer and vigilante in Hell's Kitchen. Fighting Wilson Fisk, who is now mayor of New York, in *Daredevil: Born Again*.

**The Thunderbolts / New Avengers**
Yelena Belova, Bucky, Red Guardian, U.S. Agent, Ghost and Bob (Sentry). A team of damaged ex-killers who stopped the Void and were rebranded as the "New Avengers."

**The Fantastic Four**
Reed Richards, Sue Storm, Johnny Storm and Ben Grimm from Earth-828. Stopped Galactus and the Silver Surfer in *First Steps* and are heading into *Doomsday*.

**Others**
She-Hulk, Moon Knight, Ms. Marvel, Ironheart, Echo, Werewolf by Night, Wonder Man, the Eternals and the Punisher, who shows up in *Brand New Day*.

## The Villains

### Thanos
The Mad Titan and the MCU's defining villain. Believed the universe would die unless half of all life was wiped out. Collected all six Infinity Stones and snapped away half of all life in *Infinity War*, winning outright. Killed with Stormbreaker in *Endgame*, then his past self and army were dusted by Tony Stark. Thanos is to the MCU what the Joker is to Gotham: the villain every other villain is measured against.

### The Black Order (Children of Thanos)
Ebony Maw, Proxima Midnight, Corvus Glaive and Cull Obsidian. Thanos's elite lieutenants. All killed in *Infinity War* or *Endgame*.

### Loki
Started as the God of Mischief who led the Chitauri invasion of New York. Turned hero over time.

### Ultron
Peacekeeping AI built by Tony Stark and Bruce Banner using the Mind Stone. Decided humanity was the threat. Tried to wipe out life by dropping Sokovia from the sky. His mistake gave birth to Vision.

### Hydra
Nazi science division founded by the Red Skull. Survived WWII by hiding inside S.H.I.E.L.D. for 70 years. Exposed in *The Winter Soldier*. Created the Winter Soldier program and controlled Wanda and Pietro's powers.

### Red Skull (Johann Schmidt)
Hydra's founder. Lost in WWII when he touched the Tesseract and became the guardian of the Soul Stone on Vormir.

### Helmut Zemo
Sokovian colonel who lost his family when Ultron dropped Sokovia. Tore the Avengers apart in *Civil War* without fighting them himself. Gotham's Two-Face is the closest match: a man turned by one tragedy.

### Killmonger (Erik Stevens)
Son of a Wakandan prince killed by T'Chaka. Took the Wakandan throne and tried to arm the oppressed around the world. Died refusing to live in chains.

### Hela
Goddess of death and Thor's older sister. Destroyed Mjolnir and took Asgard. Destroyed along with Asgard by Surtur in *Ragnarok*.

### Kang the Conqueror and His Variants
He Who Remains ran the single timeline from the End of Time. Loki's Sylvie killed him, which freed the multiverse and unleashed the Council of Kangs. The Kang storyline was dropped after *Quantumania* and Doctor Doom has taken over as the main multiversal threat.

### Doctor Doom (Victor von Doom)
The next big bad. Played by Robert Downey Jr. Leads the threat in *Avengers: Doomsday*.

### Wilson Fisk (Kingpin)
New York's top crime boss and now its mayor. Daredevil's eternal enemy. The MCU's closest equivalent to Penguin and Black Mask combined.

### Galactus and the Silver Surfer
The Devourer of Worlds and his herald from Earth-828. Galactus wanted Sue Storm's son Franklin. Stopped in *First Steps*.

### Gorr the God Butcher
Lost his daughter while his god mocked him. Killed gods across the universe with the Necrosword. Asked Eternity to bring back his daughter instead of destroying the gods.

### The Void (Sentry's Dark Side)
Bob Reynolds' shadow self, created through Valentina's super soldier program. Nearly swallowed New York into darkness in *Thunderbolts\**.

### Valentina Allegra de Fontaine
CIA director who builds teams of disposable operatives. The MCU's version of the corrupt system that keeps the city sick.

### Jean Grey
The main threat in *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, played by Sadie Sink. A telepathic mutant who takes over people's minds while hunting for her missing sister.

### Other Major Villains
Obadiah Stane, Whiplash, Aldrich Killian (fake Mandarin), Malekith, Ronan, Ego, Vulture, Mysterio, Ghost, Wenwu, Namor, the Leader (Samuel Sterns) and Red Hulk (Thaddeus Ross), Arishem the Celestial, Kingpin and the Hand.

## Gotham and the MCU Compared

| Element | Gotham | MCU |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | One city | Earth, space and the multiverse |
| Main hero | Batman, who never gives up | The Avengers as a team |
| Main villain | Joker, chaos | Thanos, cold purpose. Next comes Doom |
| Why the fighting never ends | The city is rotten. Batman treats symptoms | Earth draws cosmic attention. Heroes create threats |
| Hero rule | No killing | Killing is allowed and common, especially for armies |
| Grunts | Thugs who keep breaking out of Arkham | Endless armies of Chitauri, Outriders, Hydra and drones |
| Heroes who died | Jason Todd, Damian (both returned) | Tony, Natasha, Vision, T'Challa, Wanda |
| End state | Gotham is never saved | Earth is saved again and again and the threats keep growing |
