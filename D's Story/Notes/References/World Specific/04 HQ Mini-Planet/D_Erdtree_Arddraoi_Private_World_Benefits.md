# D — Erdtree + Ard-draoi + Plant 43: Benefits and Pros

**Concept:** A private world belonging to D, centered on a growing Erdtree and enriched by his druidic powers and a perfected Plant 43. There are no invading armies, rival owners, or external threats. The focus is on what he can build, grow, enjoy, and eventually do with the combination.

**Starting assets:** D already possesses a **Seed of the Erdtree** from a Legendary loot box and a **system-perfected Plant 43 seed** from the Resident Evil 2 challenge. Both are currently unplanted until HQ play. He is already an Ard-draoi with Nature Attunement, Grove Immortality, Knowledge of the Ancients, **Primal Savagery / Wild Shape** (one track), **Druid Magic**, **Bark Skin**, **Absorb Elements**, **Aeromancy** (Arcanist start), and later Bond. **Enchanting is not a starting skill.** The sections below describe the **benefits of the proposed fully developed setup**; planting and using the seeds remain D's choices. Live open = Chapter 5 HQ.

---

## Quick Glossary

### Elden Ring: the Erdtree

- **Erdtree:** An enormous golden sacred tree, a source of blessings, grace, healing, and the central symbol of a world's order.
- **Grace:** The tree's golden blessing, able to guide, restore, sustain, and protect those connected to it.
- **Site of Grace:** A resting point where health and magic are replenished and sacred flasks refill. In this private-world setup, D can establish these as a network of sanctuaries.
- **Flask of Crimson Tears:** A refillable flask that restores health.
- **Flask of Cerulean Tears:** A refillable flask that restores magic, represented by MP in D's system.
- **Golden Seed:** A sacred upgrade material that increases flask charges. It is distinct from D's Seed of the Erdtree.
- **Sacred Tear:** A sacred upgrade material that improves the amount restored by each flask use.
- **Erdtree blessing:** A gift of golden power, potentially improving vitality, stamina, resilience, and carrying capacity.
- **Golden Order:** The governing principles of the world associated with the Erdtree. In D's private-world version, this can develop into authority to establish and maintain laws within his domain.
- **Rebirth through Grace:** Returning from death at a Site of Grace. This is one of the most valuable benefits to incorporate into the completed sanctuary.

### Warcraft: World Tree and Archdruid

- **Archdruid:** A powerful druid with mastery over living nature, healing, natural magic, and animal forms.
- **World Tree:** An immense sacred tree that nourishes and protects life and can serve as a spiritual anchor for an entire land.
- **Emerald Dream:** A vast spiritual reflection of a living world, useful for exploration, meditation, and learning about nature.
- **Dreamwalking:** Entering that spiritual realm while the body rests.
- **Great Cycle:** The continuing movement of growth, death, renewal, and rebirth.
- **Druidic slumber:** Extended, voluntary sleep during which a druid can explore and learn through the Dream.

### Resident Evil 2 Remake: Plant 43

- **Plant 43:** The enormous mutated plant at the center of NEST's greenhouse. It exhibits extraordinary growth and spreads large roots, vines, and other vegetation through its environment.
- **Mother plant:** The main mass of Plant 43, which overgrew much of the facility's East Area.
- **Ivy:** The dangerous mobile, plant-infested organisms associated with the remake's Plant 43. The original Resident Evil 2 used the name Plant 43 differently, for a type of mobile plant B.O.W.; this document follows the **2019 remake** for the mother plant.
- **D's perfected Plant 43 seed:** A system-stabilized version awarded after the challenge, specifically suitable for safe cultivation. Its exciting growth traits are the attraction without requiring the greenhouse catastrophe or ordinary T-virus corruption to follow it.

## Part 1 — Benefits of the Erdtree

### A. A living heart for the world

- A magnificent golden tree at the world's center, visible over immense distances.
- A permanent home base, landmark, and meeting place with natural shelter beneath its branches.
- A vast root system that can anchor soil, support the landscape, and become the foundation of forests, lakeside groves, gardens, and settlements.
- Golden light, magical atmosphere, and a recognizable sense of safety throughout its domain.
- A sacred source of power that can grow more influential as its roots and blessings spread.

### B. Healing, recovery, and restoration

- **Sites of Grace:** Rest points throughout the domain where D can fully recover and replenish resources.
- **Health recovery:** Grace enhances the healing D already receives from druidic magic and regeneration.
- **MP recovery:** Places to restore magical reserves before more demanding work, travel, or experimentation.
- **SP recovery:** Restorative sanctuaries that support physical activity, shapeshifting, exploration, and training.
- **Sacred flasks:** Portable healing and magic restoration that recharge at Grace sites.
- **Flask improvement:** Golden Seeds can increase the number of uses; Sacred Tears can improve each use.
- **Healing for visitors and companions:** A future sanctuary where welcome guests can recover as well.

### C. Resurrection and lasting security

- **Rebirth through Grace:** If D dies, the tree's grace returns him to a chosen Site of Grace.
- A central resurrection anchor makes his private world a place he can return to even after extremely dangerous journeys.
- A network of Sites of Grace can function as multiple return points around the domain.
- The tree's protection, D's own regeneration, and his agelessness work together to make the sanctuary exceptionally durable.
- The rebirth function has special value because D's established Grove Immortality already covers aging and rapid healing: **resurrection adds something new.**

### D. Blessings and greater physical capability

- Greater health and endurance.
- Improved stamina and recovery between activities.
- Increased resilience and resistance to harmful conditions.
- Greater carrying or equipment capacity.
- An ongoing pool of sacred power that complements mana-based spells and druidic abilities.
- The potential to extend protective and restorative blessings to a chosen household or companions.

### E. Authority and control of the private domain

- D can shape the world's natural order around healthy ecosystems and sustainable growth.
- A Golden Order-style system can make his chosen rules part of the sanctuary: protected groves, controlled access, stable seasons, and sacred areas.
- With no rival ruler or outside opposition, the domain can be developed according to his plans.
- More advanced growth of the Erdtree could extend this from stewardship of the land to **direct control over the laws and magical conditions of his private world**.

## Part 3 — Plant 43: What It Adds

### What Plant 43 is in Resident Evil 2 Remake

In the remake, Plant 43 is a massive plant-based bio-organic weapon grown in NEST's East Area greenhouse. It exhibits astonishing growth, developing huge masses of vines and vegetation. Left uncontrolled, it overtook the greenhouse and nearby parts of the facility, and the Ivies associated with it became dangerous mobile threats. **That enormous growth and striking plant biology are the useful starting points for D's perfected seed.**

D's awarded version is **system-perfected and stabilized for safe cultivation**, so his version can be developed as a beneficial, deliberately managed organism rather than a laboratory outbreak.

### A. Extraordinary growth

- Grow a vast, impressive plant in far less time than conventional trees would require.
- Rapidly cover unused areas with living greenery.
- Produce massive vines, roots, canopies, climbing structures, and dense walls of vegetation.
- Rebuild damaged gardens, barriers, and natural structures with new growth.
- Use Druid Magic to guide growth toward useful shapes and locations.

### B. Living construction

- **Vine bridges** spanning ravines, rivers, and gaps.
- **Root stairways** and pathways across the landscape.
- **Living walls** around gardens, buildings, or sacred spaces.
- **Plant domes** that provide shade and shelter.
- **Platforms and elevated groves** high above the ground.
- **Root cellars and plant-covered chambers** integrated into the environment.
- **Natural gates** that open or close through controlled plant movement.

### C. A responsive defense system

Even in a world without outside threats, there is value in a living guardian for a home base and for future visitors or adventures.

- Dense vines and roots can block routes and protect restricted spaces.
- Mobile tendrils can grasp, lift, carry, or move objects.
- A perimeter of living vegetation can provide early warning when something enters an area.
- Thick growth can shelter companions and protect fragile gardens or buildings.
- If D develops it further, Plant 43-derived **Ivy-like plant guardians** could become mobile defenders, caretakers, or sentries, built around the perfected version's safe traits.
- A successful Bond offers the possibility of coordination without relying entirely on constant spellcasting.

### D. A botanical companion and ecosystem builder

- The main plant can become a distinct, living presence within D's world.
- Its growth can create greenhouses, jungle gardens, vine forests, and sheltered habitats.
- It can support climbing plants, flowering vines, mosses, and other compatible vegetation around it.
- Its branches and roots can offer places for birds, insects, and other animals introduced later.
- A bond could allow D to communicate with it, share awareness, or guide its expansion.
- As its unique traits develop, it could become a **specialized plant companion**, separate from the Erdtree's role as the world's sacred center.

### E. Botanical study, materials, and discovery

- A unique source of fantastical plant materials and structures for D to experiment with.
- Opportunities to study new forms of regeneration, vine movement, and adaptive growth within the fictional system.
- Potential to cultivate unusual flowers, fibers, sap, roots, and other magical or crafting materials.
- Interesting links to D's healing, Chef, and nature-magic abilities.
- A natural project for his interest in plants and companions.

## Part 5 — Best Arrangement for the Private World

### The central sanctuary: the Erdtree

Plant the Erdtree in a broad central grove with plenty of room for its trunk and roots. Beneath it, develop:

- A main Site of Grace and personal resting area.
- A spring, stream, or pool surrounded by flowering plants.
- A place for meditation, dreamwalking, and druidic study.
- Gardens and orchards receiving the tree's blessing.
- Paths that eventually lead to other Sites of Grace.

### The surrounding living gardens: Plant 43

Establish Plant 43 in a dedicated outer garden or greenhouse belt, where its extraordinary growth can spread in useful directions:

- Vines forming terraces, bridges, and walkways.
- Thick vegetation making natural walls and privacy screens.
- Shaded plant houses and living structures.
- A wide garden network that can expand into new land.
- Dedicated areas for future plant guardians, experimental groves, and special cultivation.

This layout lets the **Erdtree remain the sacred center** while **Plant 43 becomes the fast-growing builder and caretaker** of the surrounding landscape.

### The larger world

From the central grove, D can gradually develop:

- Vast forests, flowering meadows, and ancient-looking woodlands.
- Lakes, streams, waterfalls, and wetlands.
- Orchards, herb gardens, vegetable beds, and rare plant collections.
- Living houses, tree platforms, vine bridges, and secluded groves.
- Habitats for future animals and voluntary companions.
- A network of sacred resting places.
- Dream-linked gardens and places for spiritual exploration.

**Erdtree:** The spiritual heart of the world — grace, restoration, blessings, rebirth, dream access, and sacred authority.
