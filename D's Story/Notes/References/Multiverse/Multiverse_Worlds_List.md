# Multiverse Worlds List

*Reference list only. Numbering identifies entries; it does not establish a travel order or story progression.*

**Power:** 1 = ordinary human, 3 = trained or early tech, 5 = superhuman or cyborg, 7 = mecha or shifter, 8 = city-level, 10 = planetary
**Violence:** 1 = peaceful, 10 = constant brutal slaughter
**Tech:** Rough real-world era equivalent
