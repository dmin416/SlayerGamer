# Monster Harvest Guide

## All Ways to Cook

### Fire and Dry Heat
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Roast | Oven or hearth heat until browned, basted or wrapped in fat if lean |
| Spit Roast | Whole on a turning spit over open fire |
| Grill | Over coals or flame |
| Bake | Moderate dry oven heat |
| Clay Bake | Packed in clay, leaves or salt and buried in embers or baked |
| Smoke | Low heat and smoke for hours, often after brining |

### Wet Heat
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Boil | Cooked in boiling water |
| Simmer and Stew | Chunks cooked fully in liquid for hours |
| Braise | Seared, then simmered partly covered in wine, stock, cream or cider |
| Marinated Stew | Soaked in vinegar and wine for days, then stewed (hasenpfeffer, sauerbraten) |
| Blood Stew | Stewed in wine and thickened with the animal's blood (civet) |
| Poach | Gently simmered in broth, milk or wine |
| Steam | Cooked over boiling water, common in dumplings and buns |
| Sous Vide | Sealed and cooked in a low-temperature water bath, then seared |
| Soup and Broth | Bones, scraps and vegetables boiled down for stock or soup |
| Curry | Simmered in spiced sauce |

### Fat and Pan
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Fry | Floured or battered and pan or deep fried |
| Sauté | Quick cooked in a little fat |
| Stir-Fry | Thin strips cooked fast over very high heat |
| Confit | Slow cooked submerged in fat until fall-apart tender |

### Skewered, Ground and Shaped
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Skewers | Cubes on sticks, grilled or roasted |
| Sausage | Ground with fat and stuffed into casings |
| Meatballs and Patties | Ground and shaped, then fried or baked |
| Pâté and Terrine | Ground meat and liver baked in a mold |
| Rillettes | Confit meat shredded and packed in its fat as a spread |
| Galantine | Deboned, stuffed, rolled and cooked |

### Baked and Combined Dishes
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Pie | Filling under or inside a pastry crust |
| Casserole | Layered and baked with potatoes, beans or rice |
| Rice Dishes | Cooked in with rice (paella, pilaf, risotto) |
| Ragu | Shredded meat in tomato and wine sauce over pasta |
| Dumplings | Filling wrapped in dough, then steamed, boiled or fried |

### Preserved
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Jerky | Thin strips salted and dried |
| Salt-Cure | Salted and air dried whole or in pieces |
| Pickle | Cooked or raw food kept in vinegar brine |
| Ferment | Salted and left to sour or age |

## Harvest by Meat Type

### Small Game
**Tastes like:** Rabbit, squirrel
**Monsters:** Horned Rabbit, Wolpertinger, Giant Rat, Tanuki, Kamaitachi, Raiju, Chupacabra

- **Meat:** Saddle/loin, hind legs, front legs, ribs
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, kidneys
- **Bone and hard parts:** Skull, long bones, teeth/fangs, horns, claws
- **Other:** Pelt, fat (small amounts)

### Venison
**Tastes like:** Deer, elk
**Monsters:** Horned Deer, Peryton, Qilin, Wendigo, Questing Beast

- **Meat:** Backstrap, tenderloin, haunch, shoulder, neck, shank, ribs
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, kidneys, tongue
- **Bone and hard parts:** Antlers/horns, hooves, long bones (marrow), skull
- **Other:** Hide, sinew, tallow, blood

### Horse
**Tastes like:** Horse, lean sweet red meat
**Monsters:** Unicorn, Bicorn, Pegasus, Centaur, Hippogriff, Kelpie, Hippocamp, Nuckelavee, Tikbalang

- **Meat:** Loin, tenderloin, rump, shoulder, neck, ribs, tail
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, tongue
- **Bone and hard parts:** Horn, hooves, long bones (marrow), skull, teeth
- **Other:** Hide, mane/tail hair, fat, feathers (winged kinds)

### Beef
**Tastes like:** Beef
**Monsters:** Wild Bull, Minotaur, Gorgon (bull), Ushi-oni, Catoblepas, Lamassu

- **Meat:** Ribeye, sirloin, tenderloin, brisket, chuck, round, flank, short ribs, shank, cheek, oxtail
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, kidneys, tongue, tripe, sweetbreads, brain
- **Bone and hard parts:** Horns, hooves, long bones (marrow), knuckles, skull
- **Other:** Hide, tallow, blood, sinew, feathers (Lamassu)

### Goat and Mutton
**Tastes like:** Goat, lamb
**Monsters:** Krampus, Chimera

- **Meat:** Leg, loin, shoulder, rack/ribs, neck, shank
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, kidneys, tongue, tripe
- **Bone and hard parts:** Horns, hooves, long bones, skull
- **Other:** Hide, wool/fur, fat

### Pork
**Tastes like:** Pork, wild boar
**Monsters:** Giant Boar, Orc, Baku

- **Meat:** Loin, tenderloin, belly, shoulder, ham, ribs, cheek, jowl, trotters
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, kidneys, intestines (casings), stomach, blood
- **Bone and hard parts:** Tusks, long bones, knuckles, skull
- **Other:** Skin (crackling), lard, bristles

### Canine
**Tastes like:** Dog, wolf: strong, gamey
**Monsters:** Wolf, Dire Wolf, Warg, Hellhound, Barghest, Cerberus, Amarok, Fenrir, Werewolf, Gnoll, Kobold, Leucrotta, Kitsune, Ahuizotl

- **Meat:** Loin, haunch, shoulder, ribs
- **Organs:** Heart, liver (avoid in large beasts)
- **Bone and hard parts:** Fangs, claws, skull, long bones
- **Other:** Pelt, fat, fire glands (Hellhound)

### Big Cat
**Tastes like:** Lean, pork-like, faintly gamey
**Monsters:** Nekomata, Manticore, Sphinx, Nian, Pixiu, Bai Ze, Nue, Griffon, Taotie

- **Meat:** Loin, haunch, shoulder, ribs, tail (Manticore: venom-free cuts only)
- **Organs:** Heart, liver
- **Bone and hard parts:** Fangs, claws, skull, long bones, tail spikes
- **Other:** Pelt, mane, feathers (Griffon), venom sac (Manticore)

### Bear and Ape
**Tastes like:** Bear: rich, fatty, dark
**Monsters:** Giant Bear, Yeti, Yowie, Giant Ape, Mapinguari, Bugbear

- **Meat:** Backstrap, haunch, shoulder, ribs, paws
- **Organs:** Heart, liver (toxic in large amounts)
- **Bone and hard parts:** Claws, fangs, skull, long bones
- **Other:** Pelt, bear fat (grease), gallbladder

### Fowl
**Tastes like:** Chicken, duck, game bird
**Monsters:** Birds, Cockatrice, Basan, Harpy, Siren, Crebain, Strix, Tengu, Roc, Thunderbird, Garuda, Anzu, Simurgh, Ziz, Hraesvelgr, Phoenix, Kunpeng

- **Meat:** Breast, thigh, drumstick, wing
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, gizzard
- **Bone and hard parts:** Beak, talons, wishbone, hollow long bones, skull
- **Other:** Feathers, eggs, skin, fat, fire organ (Phoenix, Basan), lightning gland (Thunderbird)

### Bat
**Tastes like:** Dark, gamey, small yield
**Monsters:** Giant Bat, Camazotz, Ahool

- **Meat:** Breast, thighs
- **Organs:** Heart, liver
- **Bone and hard parts:** Fangs, wing bones, claws, skull
- **Other:** Wing membrane, fur, fat

### Lizard and Snake
**Tastes like:** Chicken, white fish, alligator
**Monsters:** Lizardman, Giant Lizard, Giant Snake, Basilisk, Salamander, Tsuchinoko, Amphisbaena, Naga, Lamia, Medusa, Ammit, Sea Serpent, Lindworm, Were-worm

- **Meat:** Tail, backstrap, body sections, legs
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, eggs
- **Bone and hard parts:** Fangs, skull, ribs, vertebrae, claws
- **Other:** Scales, skin/leather, venom glands, eyes (Basilisk), fire gland (Salamander)

### Dragon
**Tastes like:** Beef and alligator, very rich
**Monsters:** Dragon, Wyvern, Hydra, Fell Beast, Cuélebre, Yamata no Orochi, Vritra, Bakunawa, Nidhogg, Tiamat, Bahamut, Jörmungandr, Ouroboros, Apophis, Typhon, Tarasque

- **Meat:** Tail, backstrap, haunch, shoulder, ribs, neck, wing muscle
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, tongue, breath gland, blood
- **Bone and hard parts:** Fangs, claws, horns, skull, ribs, vertebrae, wing bones, long bones (marrow)
- **Other:** Scales, hide, wing membrane, blood, eyes, eggs, venom (Wyvern, Hydra)

### Turtle
**Tastes like:** Veal, chicken, a little fishy
**Monsters:** Giant Tortoise, Adamantoise, Zaratan, Kappa

- **Meat:** Leg, neck, tail, body meat
- **Organs:** Liver, eggs
- **Bone and hard parts:** Shell/carapace, plastron, beak, skull, claws
- **Other:** Calipash (shell gelatin), fat, scales

### Frog
**Tastes like:** Chicken, mild
**Monsters:** Giant Toad, Vodyanoy

- **Meat:** Legs, back
- **Organs:** Liver, eggs
- **Bone and hard parts:** Skeleton, skull
- **Other:** Skin (slime/poison glands), tongue

### Fish
**Tastes like:** White or oily fish
**Monsters:** Giant Catfish, Giant Eel, Land Shark, Fishman, Mermaid, Grindylow

- **Meat:** Fillets, belly, cheeks, collar, fins (shark), tail
- **Organs:** Liver, roe
- **Bone and hard parts:** Spine, ribs, skull, teeth
- **Other:** Skin, scales, oil, swim bladder

### Whale and Marine Mammal
**Tastes like:** Whale, seal: dark, oily
**Monsters:** Giant Whale, Bake-kujira, Leviathan, Umibozu, Selkie, Encantado, Bunyip

- **Meat:** Red meat loin, tail, flippers, belly
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, tongue
- **Bone and hard parts:** Jawbone, ribs, vertebrae, baleen/teeth, skull
- **Other:** Blubber, skin, oil, ambergris

### Large Herbivore
**Tastes like:** Elephant, tough beef
**Monsters:** Oliphaunt, Behemoth, Mokele-mbembe

- **Meat:** Trunk, loin, haunch, shoulder, feet
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, tongue
- **Bone and hard parts:** Tusks/ivory, horns, long bones (marrow), skull
- **Other:** Hide, fat

### Cephalopod
**Tastes like:** Calamari, octopus
**Monsters:** Giant Octopus, Kraken, Watcher in the Water, Scylla, Charybdis

- **Meat:** Tentacles, mantle
- **Organs:** Liver, roe
- **Bone and hard parts:** Beak, cuttlebone/pen
- **Other:** Ink sac, suckers, skin

### Shellfish
**Tastes like:** Crab, shrimp, clam, escargot
**Monsters:** Giant Shrimp, Giant Crab, Giant Clam, Giant Snail, Mimic

- **Meat:** Claw meat, leg meat, tail meat, body meat, foot (clam, snail, mimic)
- **Organs:** Tomalley, roe
- **Bone and hard parts:** Shell, claws, pearls (clam)
- **Other:** Chitin plates, slime (snail)

### Insect and Arachnid
**Tastes like:** Shrimp, nutty
**Monsters:** Killer Bee, Giant Spider, Giant Scorpion, Giant Ant, Giant Beetle, Giant Mantis, Giant Centipede, Giant Moth, Giant Fly, Giant Mosquito, Giant Cockroach, Arachne, Jorogumo, Tsuchigumo, Scorpion Man

- **Meat:** Leg meat, thorax meat, abdomen meat, larvae
- **Organs:** Venom-free organs, eggs
- **Bone and hard parts:** Exoskeleton/chitin plates, mandibles, stingers, claws
- **Other:** Silk, honey and royal jelly (Killer Bee), venom sacs, wings

### Worm
**Tastes like:** Squid, earthy
**Monsters:** Giant Worm, Sandworm, Giant Leech

- **Meat:** Body segments
- **Organs:** Gizzard
- **Bone and hard parts:** Teeth (Sandworm)
- **Other:** Hide, slime, blood (Leech)

### Jelly and Slime
**Tastes like:** Jelly, agar, savory jelly
**Monsters:** Slime, Giant Jellyfish, Kesaran-pasaran

- **Meat:** Gel body
- **Organs:** Core
- **Bone and hard parts:** Crystallized core
- **Other:** Stinging tentacles (dried), magic core

### Plant and Fungus
**Tastes like:** Mushroom, root vegetable
**Monsters:** Walking Mushroom, Mandrake, Man-Eating Plant, Treant, Huorn, Dryad, Kodama, Leshy

- **Meat:** Caps, stems, roots, fruit
- **Organs:** Seed pods, root nodules, fruiting bodies
- **Bone and hard parts:** Heartwood, bark
- **Other:** Sap, spores, leaves, seeds

### Humanoid
**Tastes like:** Pork and veal (long pork); Troll and Ogre are tough and gamey
**Monsters:** Goblin, Hobgoblin, Dark Elf, Gnome, Ogre, Troll, Cyclops, Ettin, Giant, Titan, Oni, Uruk-hai, Fomorian, Redcap, Boggart, Brownie, Duende, Hag, Kikimora, Bannik, Hitotsume-kozo, Nopperabo, Akaname, Rokurokubi, Yuki-onna, Doppelganger, Changeling, Gremlin, Mewlips

- **Meat:** Loin, thigh, shoulder, ribs, belly, cheek, rump
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, kidneys, tongue, brain, Troll blood and heart (regeneration)
- **Bone and hard parts:** Skull, long bones (marrow), teeth, horns (Oni), single eye (Cyclops)
- **Other:** Hide, fat, blood, ears

### Fiend
**Tastes like:** Spicy, smoky, charred meat with a sulfur bite
**Monsters:** Imp, Succubus, Rakshasa, Djinn, Fallen Angel, Balrog, Demon Lord, Rahu, Aswang, Manananggal

- **Meat:** Loin, haunch, wing muscle, tail, ribs
- **Organs:** Heart, liver, demon core, fire gland (Balrog)
- **Bone and hard parts:** Horns, claws, fangs, wings, skull
- **Other:** Blood, ash, feathers (Fallen Angel), hide

### Undead
**Tastes like:** Dry-aged and cured meat; Mummy eats like jerky, Vampire like pale lean veal
**Monsters:** Skeleton, Zombie, Ghoul, Mummy, Jiangshi, Revenant, Draugr, Death Knight, Lich, Dullahan, Vampire, Barrow-wight, Gashadokuro, Army of the Dead, Nazgûl, Rusalka

- **Meat:** Dry-aged flesh (Zombie, Ghoul, Draugr), cured flesh (Mummy), lean pale flesh (Vampire, Jiangshi)
- **Organs:** Heart, blood (Vampire), Lich phylactery
- **Bone and hard parts:** Bones for broth and marrow, skull, teeth, giant bones (Gashadokuro)
- **Other:** Bone dust, grave wrappings, cursed relics, armor

### Spirit and Elemental
**Tastes like:** Cool, fizzy and airy, like whipped jelly; Undine tastes like spring water, Fairy like honeyed songbird
**Monsters:** Will-o'-the-Wisp, Ghost, Wraith, Banshee, Shade, Poltergeist, Grim Reaper, Gaki, Undine, Fairy

- **Meat:** Ectoplasm (gelatinous), condensed essence, Fairy body meat
- **Organs:** Spirit core
- **Bone and hard parts:** Crystallized essence, Fairy bones and wings
- **Other:** Fairy dust, wisp flame, ectoplasm residue

### Construct
**Tastes like:** Mineral, earthy and salty; Homunculus is soft alchemical flesh like veal
**Monsters:** Golem, Living Armor, Gargoyle, Colossus, Mudman, Scarecrow, Living Doll, Tsukumogami, Kasa-obake, Wanyudo, Homunculus

- **Meat:** Clay flesh (Mudman, Golem), straw (Scarecrow), wood and lacquer (Tsukumogami, Kasa-obake), alchemical flesh (Homunculus)
- **Organs:** Magic core
- **Bone and hard parts:** Stone and metal (mineral salt), clay, wood, wheel (Wanyudo)
- **Other:** Enchanted parts, armor plates, alchemical fluid

## Types of Alcohol

### Brewed from Grain
Starch is turned to sugar (malting, mold or chewing), then fermented.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Beer and Ale | Malted barley, wheat or rye with hops or herbs |
| Rice Wine | Rice converted with koji mold (sake, makgeolli, huangjiu) |
| Chicha | Corn |
| Kvass | Rye bread |

### Fermented from Fruit
Crushed fruit juice fermented by yeast.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Wine | Grapes or other fruit |
| Cider | Apples |
| Perry | Pears |

### Fermented from Sugar, Sap or Milk
Natural sugars fermented without brewing or crushing.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Mead | Honey and water |
| Palm Wine | Palm tree sap |
| Pulque | Agave sap |
| Kumis | Mare's milk |

### Fortified
Fermented drink with distilled spirit added.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Fortified Wine | Wine strengthened with brandy (port, sherry) |

### Distilled from Grain or Starch
Brewed mash boiled and condensed into spirit.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Whiskey | Grain mash, aged in wood |
| Moonshine | Unaged grain or sugar spirit |
| Vodka | Grain or potatoes, filtered clear |
| Baijiu | Sorghum |
| Soju and Shochu | Rice, barley or sweet potato |

### Distilled with Botanicals
Grain spirit redistilled or steeped with herbs and spices.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Gin | Juniper |
| Aquavit | Caraway or dill |
| Absinthe | Wormwood and anise |

### Distilled from Fruit
Wine or fermented fruit distilled.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Brandy | Grapes or other fruit (cognac, eau-de-vie) |

### Distilled from Sugar or Plant Sap
Fermented sugarcane, agave or sap distilled.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Rum | Sugarcane or molasses |
| Tequila and Mezcal | Agave |
| Arrack | Palm sap, rice or sugarcane |

### Infused and Sweetened
Spirit soaked with flavorings, often sweetened.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Liqueur | Spirit with sugar and fruit, herbs or cream |
| Bitters | Spirit infused with roots, bark and herbs |

### Mixed and Heated
Finished drinks combined or warmed.
| Type | Made From |
|---|---|
| Mulled Drinks | Wine, cider or ale heated with spices |
| Punch | Spirit or wine mixed with fruit and sugar |

## Ways of Cooking Vegetables

### Raw and Cold
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Raw | Sliced, shredded or whole in salads and slaws |
| Marinated | Raw vegetables soaked in acid, oil and seasoning |

### Water and Steam
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Boiled | Cooked in salted boiling water |
| Blanched | Brief boil then ice water, keeps color and crunch |
| Steamed | Cooked over boiling water |
| Poached | Gently simmered in broth, wine or butter |
| Sous Vide | Sealed and cooked in a low-temperature water bath |

### Dry Heat
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Roasted | High oven heat until browned and caramelized |
| Baked | Moderate oven heat, often whole (potatoes, squash) |
| Grilled | Over coals or flame |
| Charred | Direct flame until blackened skin (peppers, eggplant) |
| Ember Roasted | Buried in coals or ash |
| Salt Baked | Encased in salt crust and baked |
| Smoked | Low smoke until infused |

### Fat and Pan
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Sautéed | Quick cooked in a little fat over medium-high heat |
| Stir-Fried | Small pieces tossed fast over very high heat |
| Fried | Battered or plain, pan or deep fried (tempura, fritters, chips) |
| Caramelized | Slow cooked in fat until deep brown and sweet (onions) |
| Confit | Slow cooked submerged in fat |
| Glazed | Cooked in butter, sugar and liquid until shiny |

### Slow and Wet
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Braised | Browned, then simmered partly covered in liquid |
| Stewed | Simmered fully in liquid |
| Creamed | Cooked in cream or white sauce (creamed spinach) |
| Soup | Simmered in stock, chunky or blended |

### Baked Dishes
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Gratin | Layered with cream or cheese and baked until crusted |
| Stuffed | Hollowed and filled, then baked (peppers, squash) |
| Casserole | Mixed with sauce or starch and baked |

### Mashed and Blended
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Mashed | Cooked soft and crushed |
| Puréed | Cooked and blended smooth |

### Preserved
| Method | How |
|---|---|
| Pickled | Soaked in vinegar brine |
| Fermented | Salted and left to sour (sauerkraut, kimchi) |
| Dried | Sun, air or low heat dehydrated |
| Candied | Simmered in sugar syrup and dried |
| Salted | Packed in salt |

## Common Seasonings and Herbs
