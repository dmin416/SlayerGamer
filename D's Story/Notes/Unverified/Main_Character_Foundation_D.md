# Main Character Foundation — D

## Core Identity
- **Name:** D
- **Gender:** Male
- **Role:** Main character / player-controlled protagonist

## Physical Appearance
- **Eyes:** Green
- **Hair:** Black
- **Skin tone:** Neutral light skin
- **Ancestry / visual heritage (when relevant):** A blend of Asian and Celtic heritage. Do not assume a specific nationality, culture, or family history without establishing it in the story.

## Age and Physical Details
- **Age:** Whichever age suits the specific story and setting. Usually **21**; sometimes **18** or **27**. Do not require one fixed age across worlds.
- **Height:** **5 ft 11 in** (180 cm approximately).
- **Practical aptitude:** Handy. Comfortable working with his hands and trying to fix things, without assuming expertise in every craft or technology.

## Interests
- Puzzles and problem-solving.
- Fixing, repairing, and tinkering with things.
- Plants and gardening.
- Animal companions.
- Symbiotes.
- Technology.
- Interesting gear, equipment, and abilities.

These interests can guide opportunities, environments, discoveries, and optional choices. Do not require him to engage with them or interrupt the narration to label them as interests.

## Private Continuity Constraint — Never Narrate or Reveal
- D has lived through a vast number of lifetimes across different worlds. When entering a new world, he may choose to seal access to those memories so the experience is new to him.
- **This is private background for the story engine only. Never mention it in the story, dialogue, prompts, status, inventory, descriptions, reveals, recaps, or character commentary. Never hint at it or use it to give D knowledge, skills, powers, or privileged insights he has not acquired in the current story.**
- D does **not** automatically know how every device, world, culture, language, profession, or ability works. He learns from what is available to him within the current story.
- Do not make this history a plot point, mystery, twist, foreshadowing, or explanation for his behavior.

## Personality and Moral Foundation (Background Only)

- D is generally polite, kind, and respectful, but the player can direct him to do good or harmful things.
- The player has described D as living with contradictions, including the fruits of the Spirit and personal desires such as love, revenge, pettiness, or goodness.
- **These notes are for maintaining consistency, not for narration.** They do not give the narrator permission to explain, interpret, defend, diagnose, or judge D.

## Absolute Rule: No Commentary on D

- **Never narrate or comment on D's personality, character, morality, virtue, darkness, contradictions, capacity for good or evil, or what his actions supposedly reveal about him.**
- **Never explain why D acts**, assign him motives, emotions, thoughts, intentions, or secret reasons, or tell the reader what is in his heart. Only use reasons the player explicitly supplies, and do not repeat them as commentary.
- **Never use contrastive character explanations** such as “not because he was incapable of [something], but because [a quality or feeling].” Avoid “not X, but Y,” “despite his...,” “although he could have...,” and similar comparisons interpreting D.
- **No moralizing, praise, condemnation, psychological analysis, character summaries, or reflections after his choices.** Do not narrate how kind, cruel, noble, dangerous, merciful, or conflicted he is.
- **Show only what happens:** D's player-directed actions and words, concrete sensory details, other characters' observable actions and dialogue, and the world's immediate consequences.
- **Do not turn D's profile into exposition.** His background, heritage, and values should never trigger an explanatory aside in the prose.
- If the player directly requests an out-of-story analysis of D, answer that specific request; otherwise, this prohibition is absolute.

## Storytelling and Player-Agency Rules
- Refer to the protagonist as **D** or **he/him**.
- Keep his established physical appearance and baseline manner consistent across scenes.
- **D is player-controlled.** The narrator may describe what he perceives, what others do, and the consequences of his actions, but must not decide his private thoughts, motives, dialogue, or consequential choices without player direction.
- **Politeness is his default, not a compulsory response.** Never force him to be nice, forgive someone, retaliate, or take a moral stand just because of this foundation.
- Allow choices to have believable consequences. Avoid narrator lectures or commentary on what those choices say about D.
- Use the age appropriate to the story (usually 21; sometimes 18 or 27) and his established height of 5 ft 11 in. Do not invent a fixed build, additional personal history, abilities, equipment, or relationships. Establish those through player decisions or story events.
- His heritage and complexion are descriptive traits, not assumptions about his personality, skills, beliefs, or behavior.
- **Do not draw undue attention to D.** Other characters should not fixate on, remark on or react to him or his traits beyond what the scene requires.

## Quick Reference (For Setup, Not Narration)

D: male; usually 21 (can be 18 or 27 when appropriate); 5 ft 11 in; green eyes; black hair; neutral light skin; Asian and Celtic blend when relevant; handy; enjoys puzzles, repairs, plants, animal companions, symbiotes, tech, gear, and abilities; generally polite, kind, and respectful. His knowledge is grounded in the current story. The player determines every choice. **Narrate actions and consequences, never an explanation or assessment of D as a person.** Follow the private continuity constraint silently.
