## Defender Perk

- D can **voluntarily intercept damage meant for one chosen person**, receiving the transferred damage himself.
- D's normal protections, resistances and regeneration operate against the transferred damage.
- Activated and deactivated at D's discretion; only **one chosen protected target at a time** at current rank.
- The perk does not impose D's choice on a target or automatically intercept damage without activation.
- Any expansion to multiple targets or additional functions requires future progression; none is currently granted.

## Headquarters unlock

- **Multiverse Headquarters is unlocked** as a permanent base option for D and his household, companions, vehicles, research and future travel.
- **Site, layout, facilities, access rules and expansion mechanics remain undecided.** Do not assume an established building, dimension, workshop or completed HQ interior until D chooses and the story establishes it.
- The unlock is distinct from Red Queen's access to OCP infrastructure.
