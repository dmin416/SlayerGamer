# Common Fantasy Monsters by Rough Difficulty

Numbers after each name are the range of tiers it can reasonably appear at.

## Tier Guide

| Tier | Name | Threat | Who Handles It |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fodder | Pests and starter kills. Dangerous only in swarms or to the untrained. | Villagers with tools, new adventurers, kids with sticks in a pinch |
| 2 | Low | Real threat to a lone fighter. Raids farms, ambushes travelers, forms packs and tribes. | Trained guards, rookie adventurer parties, hunters |
| 3 | Mid | Can wipe out a patrol or terrorize a village. Often has a special ability or trick. | Veteran adventurers, knight squads, town militia with a plan |
| 4 | High | Regional disaster. Levels towns, sinks ships, needs a coordinated effort to bring down. | Elite parties, guild-level raids, small armies |
| 5 | Boss | Kingdom or world-ending. Legendary, often unique, the subject of prophecies and final arcs. | Heroes, legendary parties, whole nations, gods |
