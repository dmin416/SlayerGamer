## Learning He Is a Multiversal Traveler

Not on the page yet. The two facts about him: a memory seal keeps each new world fresh, and he can take every memory out with him. He is reluctant to share because of caution, not because the seal stops him.

**Outcome: She accepts him**, after a testing phase.

**Why acceptance**

- **Consistency over words:** Parents said they would be home. They were at the lab. She believes repeated showing up.
- **Safe contradiction:** He has power and does not use her as a sample or a ticket. The multiverse deepens that, since he could leave and keeps choosing to stay.
- **Rebel loyalty:** Once she decides he is pack, walking away would be her becoming her parents.

**Her reaction**

- Suspicion first. A hidden truth feels like another adult project she was not told about.
- Distance, jokes, and tests to see whether he gets angry or tries to manage her.
- A drop in tension once he stays steady and answers what he can.

**Does she sense his reservations?**

Yes. She grew up around people who paused before the real sentence. She reads hesitation as "there is a lab behind this." The magician approach converts that from a Birkin secret into a game she can step into.

**Memory seal**

The seal fits the magician approach: he keeps each world playful and never fully hides what he is. Because the memories come out with him, nothing about his past is truly closed to her. Once she understands the seal exists for fun, it registers as a game she is invited into rather than a file locked in NEST.

**The magician approach**

With her he acts like a magician and never fully hides it.

- Small tricks and half-reveals appear naturally in daily life.
- She always has a chance to notice and ask, so nothing is ever hidden and then exposed.
- The approach keeps her in control of how much she learns and when.
- It turns his secret from an ambush into a game she can step into at her own pace.
- When the full truth arrives it confirms what she already half knew, which is the opposite of being the last Birkin in the room.

**What would pull her away:** he used the multiverse to manipulate her, hid it in a way that endangered her, she learns it through exposure rather than his own disclosure, or he treats her as a resource rather than a partner.

**What wins her:** consistency, and the magician approach so the truth never arrives as an ambush.
