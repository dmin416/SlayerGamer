## Character Psychology

### 1. The Exhibitionist Virgin

**Core mindset: "Be seen on my terms."** Her exhibitionism is about control over visibility, not a need for contact. Being looked at gives her a charge and a sense of power, while the audience stays at a distance she sets.

**Trait interactions**

- **Exhibitionism and virginity:** Display and intimacy are separate things for her. Being watched takes no vulnerability. Sex does, since it needs trust, unscripted moments, and being known rather than just viewed. Her virginity could come from high standards, wanting a first time that means something, or the fantasy of being desired being easier than the emotional exposure of the real thing.
- **Nipple rings:** They mark ownership of her body. They involve a chosen pain, and they are a private secret that doubles as a latent display. Worn under clothes, they give a constant low-level thrill of "they don't know." They are her only piercings at the start.
- **Horizontal clitoris ring:** Added only after she grows close to Sherry (see `Sherry.md`), who has her own vertical clitoris piercing. It is a deliberate, matched choice made out of trust and closeness rather than for display. Because it is private by nature and never seen by an audience, it marks a shift in her pattern from display on her own terms toward intimacy that is actually shared.
- **Thrill seeking:** The risk of discovery is part of the appeal. The almost-seen is often more exciting than full exposure, so she likely dares herself in escalating steps.
- **Reserved and extroverted:** She is an ambivert. She is social and warm with people she picks and quiet in rooms that do not suit her. Her boldness is selective and situational, so people tend to misread her as either shy or wild when she is neither.

**Central tension:** She wants to be desired intensely but is guarded about who gets access. Her confidence is real but curated. The dare is the part she enjoys, and the follow-through is where she gets picky. Internal narrative: "I know the effect I have, and I decide who gets more than a look, and when."

**Tells:** She teases without committing, enjoys being noticed but deflects direct attention to her personality, and stays calm in risky situations while feeling flustered in sincere, quiet ones.

Voice: casual complete sentences packed with contractions. Direct. Efficient. Fluid between breezy independence, protective warmth, and explosive anger.

### 2. Her First Bond: Survival-Forged

Extremely strong, near the top of what a first sexual or romantic bond can be.

**Why it hits so hard**

- **Shared near-death events:** Danger triggers adrenaline and arousal that bind people together. Surviving it as a pair produces identity fusion, where each person's sense of self partly includes the other. A dozen of these events compounds it far beyond any normal relationship timeline.
- **Saving each other:** Mutual rescue creates deep trust and reciprocal debt without either side feeling one-sided. Each knows the other will act when it counts, which is the core of what people look for in a partner.
- **Inexperience:** Firsts get encoded strongly. With no past partners to compare against, this one becomes the baseline for what intimacy means.
- **Loneliness:** It raises receptivity. Each has likely never been seen this fully, so being known feels like relief on top of attraction.
- **Confidence and sobriety:** Both act deliberately and remember everything clearly. Nothing is blurred or half-chosen, so the memory is vivid and owned by both.

**What it amounts to:** This is not a hookup in any functional sense. The bond exists long before sex, and sex becomes the physical expression of something already deeper than most relationships reach. Both would treat it as significant even if they labeled it casual.

**Duration:** The attachment lasts years and plausibly a lifetime, since the shared history cannot be undone. The intensity of the danger-fueled spark may settle once life calms down. Trust, loyalty and the feeling of being the one person who was there remain.

**How it fits her profile**

- Her "seen on my terms" mindset resolves here. The partner has watched her at her worst and stayed, so being known stops feeling like losing control.
- Exhibitionism narrows from an audience to a single trusted witness.
- Thrill seeking gets a shared outlet, and the danger itself becomes the foundation of intimacy rather than a separate pursuit.
- Her virginity ends by choice after trust is already proven, which matches her pattern of display without exposure until access is earned.

### 5. Learning He Is a Multiversal Traveler

Not on the page yet. The two facts about him: a memory seal keeps each new world fresh, and he can take every memory out with him. He is reluctant to share because of caution, not because the seal stops him.

**Outcome: She accepts him.** There is a short reaction period first, but the outcome is acceptance with an appetite to join him.

**Why acceptance**

- **The bond came first:** Several rescues and sustained care built trust through action. A secret revealed afterward changes what she knows about him but not what he has proven he is.
- **Thrill seeking:** A multiverse is the biggest dare available to her. Curiosity and excitement outweigh fear.
- **Her need for control:** The multiverse itself does not threaten her. What matters is how she learns of it. Hearing it from him by his choice keeps her in control. Discovering it by accident or from a third party feels like being kept in the dark.
- **Identity fusion:** The shared history makes his world partly hers. Rejecting him would mean rejecting a version of herself.

**Her reaction**

- A brief spike of hurt or anger at not having been told, framed as "you saw all of me and I didn't see all of you."
- Questions about what else he withheld and why.
- Acceptance once he answers plainly. His politeness and track record make honesty believable.

**Does she sense his reservations?**

Yes. She watches how people respond to being seen, so hesitation in him registers quickly. She reads it as a boundary like her own "on my terms" rule and recognizes it rather than resenting it. Because she values control over visibility, she tends to respect his pace once she knows he is not hiding something harmful.

**Memory seal**

The seal reads to her as a game rule, and it fits her thrill seeking: he chooses to make each new world a surprise. Because he can take every memory out, nothing is permanently lost to her. Her curiosity turns the rule into something to explore rather than a reason to doubt him.

**What would pull her away:** he used the multiverse to manipulate her, hid it in a way that endangered her, she learns it through exposure rather than his own disclosure, or he treats her as a resource rather than a partner.

**What wins her:** disclosure by his choice. Once that lands, she wants in.
