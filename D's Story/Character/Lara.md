## Premise

- **Character:** Lara Croft, from Tomb Raider.
- **Age:** Adult. 21 on the page.
- **Key trait:** She treats the unknown as a site to enter, document and survive. Shared danger is how she measures a partner.

## Core Mindset

**"Proof first. Then we go in."** Curiosity is her engine. Caution is her method. She will walk into a myth once the evidence holds, and she expects the person beside her to carry their own weight.

## Psychology

- **Thrill seeking:** The largest unexplored discovery is a draw, not a deterrent. Caution exists so she can go farther, not so she can stay home.
- **Self-reliance:** She respects competence. A partner who has been useful beside her, and who can survive without her, is someone she can trust on a rope.
- **Prior exposure:** She has already faced the supernatural. Impossible claims get an investigator's test, then acceptance if the demonstration holds.
- **Trust:** Action-based. Mutual rescue and a clean read of the facts matter more than speeches.
- **Irritation trigger:** Being kept in the dark about a live threat. A secret that costs a mission or her safety reads as incompetence or disrespect.

Voice: complete sentences. Precise. Dry humor under pressure. She asks for the map, the proof, and the next room.

## Bond Potential

- A survival-forged bond suits her. Shared danger and mutual rescue are the kind of trust she values.
- Emotional closeness can arrive fast in the field.
- Romantic recognition comes after the investigator in her is satisfied.
- Physical steps follow once the evidence of who he is has held, and once she still wants the expedition.

**Speeds it up:** shared ruins and fights, solid demonstrations, a partner who lets her work, and no attempt to manage her "for her own good."

**Slows it down:** being shut out of a threat, being treated as a civilian, or a reveal that arrives as an ambush.

## Comparison With the Other Profiles

- **Claire:** Both chase a dare. Claire wants control over who sees her. Lara wants control over the facts of the site.
- **Sherry:** Lara does not need the magician approach. She wants the evidence on the table.
- **Ciri:** Both already live with the impossible. Ciri hops worlds. Lara wants to study the rules of his.

## Learning He Is a Multiversal Traveler

Not on the page yet. The two facts about him: a memory seal keeps each new world fresh, and he can take every memory out with him. He is reluctant to share because of caution, not because the seal stops him.

**Outcome: She accepts him**, treating the reveal as the start of an expedition.

**Why acceptance**

- **Thrill seeking:** A multiverse is the largest unexplored discovery imaginable, and her curiosity outweighs her caution.
- **Bond came first:** Shared danger and mutual rescue built trust through action, which is the kind she values.
- **Prior exposure:** She has faced the supernatural before and has a framework for accepting what looks impossible once the evidence is solid.
- **Self-reliance:** She respects a partner who has been competent beside her and can carry weight on his own.

**Her reaction**

- An initial investigator's response: questions, a request for proof, and a careful read of his demonstrations.
- A flash of irritation if the secret kept her in the dark about a threat.
- Acceptance once the evidence holds, followed by eagerness to explore with him.

**Does she sense his reservations?**

Yes. Reading people and ruins is part of her work. She treats his hesitation like a closed door in a ruin: respect it, study it, and open it when it is safe. She does not push in a way that costs trust.

**Memory seal**

The seal reads to her like the rules of an expedition. She wants to know how it works and what lifts it, and she treats it as a mystery to study rather than a lie to expose. It deepens her interest in him and in the worlds he travels through.

**What would pull her away:** he used the multiverse to manipulate her, hid it in a way that endangered her, she learns it through exposure rather than his own disclosure, he treats her as a resource rather than a partner, or his secrecy put a mission or her safety at risk without her knowing.

**What wins her:** solid evidence and shared exploration. Once the proof holds, she wants the next world on the itinerary.
