## Premise

- **Character:** Cirilla Fiona Elen Riannon, Ciri, from The Witcher.
- **Age:** Adult. 21 on the page.
- **Status:** Has learned of the multiverse.
- **Key trait:** Bearer of the Elder Blood, able to open portals between places and worlds.

## Core Mindset

**"I'm not a prize or a weapon. I choose where I go."** Her whole life has been people trying to own, hunt or use her for the Elder Blood. Freedom is her deepest need, and anything that feels like a cage triggers her.

## Psychology

- **Trauma:** Years of being chased, captured and hurt (the Wild Hunt, Bonhart, the Rats) left her hypervigilant and fast to violence. Her calm is practiced rather than natural.
- **Guilt:** She carries the weight of people who died around her. She hides it behind dry humor and competence.
- **Trust:** Slow and action-based. She believes what people do, not what they say. Those who stayed through danger (Geralt, Yennefer, the Rats she still mourns) hold enormous weight.
- **Self-image:** Capable and wary of being seen as only the Child of Destiny. She resents being valued for her blood.
- **Competence under pressure:** Her courage is real and constant. Her softness shows only with people who have earned it.

## Multiverse Effect

- She already hopped worlds through the Elder Blood, so learning of the multiverse confirms what she half knew rather than shocking her.
- It reads as freedom first: places where nobody is hunting her.
- It carries a new pressure too. Her power matters on a scale beyond one continent, which threatens her independence.
- Expect curiosity and restlessness, along with caution about anyone who wants to use the knowledge.

## Piercings

Both are private, self-chosen markers of body ownership. After years of being hunted and used for her blood, deciding what happens to her body is a quiet act of reclaiming it. Adult versions of Claire Redfield and Sherry Birkin are assumed. Claire is 21. Sherry is 18.

- **One nipple piercing (start):** Her first and only piercing. It is a private mark she gives herself, hidden under armor and clothing, with no audience.
- **Second nipple piercing (after forming a connection with Claire and Sherry):** Added once she bonds with both. It reads as a marker of belonging she chose rather than a symbol of ownership by anyone. Claire offers protection without possessiveness, and Sherry shares the experience of being targeted for what she carries, which gives Ciri the rare feeling of being understood without having to explain. The pair completing the set marks her moving from a lone guarded survivor to someone with chosen family.
- **Clitoris piercing (after the bond with Claire and Sherry deepens):** Added later, following the same pattern of a private, self-chosen marker that comes only after trust is established. It is the most private piece, never seen by anyone outside her closest circle. It marks her taking ownership of her own body and intimacy on her terms, now from a place of safety rather than defense.

**Arc use:** The single piercing shows her independence. The second shows that she can let people in without losing her freedom, which is the shift her whole arc points toward.

## Bond Potential

- A survival-forged bond suits her best, since trust comes from shared danger and mutual rescue.
- Emotional closeness can arrive fast once someone proves they stay.
- Romantic and physical steps come slower and on her terms. She needs room to leave, and any hint of possession backfires.
- She tends to initiate by action: fighting beside someone, covering their back, staying close after a hard fight.

**Speeds it up:** shared danger, a partner who respects her freedom, repeated proof that they stay, and no interest in her blood or power.

**Slows it down:** possessiveness, attempts to manage or protect her against her will, and any sign the partner values her for the Elder Blood.

## Progression Estimate

- **Emotional closeness:** Fast after a few mutual rescues.
- **Recognition of feelings:** Quick to feel and slow to name. She may doubt it as adrenaline at first.
- **First physical step:** Often through action and proximity rather than words. She may initiate once she feels safe and unpossessed.
- **Intimacy:** Comes after trust is confirmed and freedom is secure. The pace follows how fast the narrative can drive.

## Comparison With the Other Profiles

- **Claire (exhibitionist virgin):** Ciri is far less display-driven and far more guarded about being owned.
- **Sherry:** Both were treated as what they carry. Ciri already has a sword and a path. Sherry is weak until the virus, then has to live with power she did not train for.
- **Lara Croft:** Both treat impossible things as workable once the evidence holds. Ciri already hops worlds. Lara wants to map them.

## Learning He Is a Multiversal Traveler

Not on the page yet. She already knows the multiverse exists through the Elder Blood. This section is the later beat: learning that the partner who entered her heart travels the same way, with a memory seal that keeps each world fresh. He can take every memory out with him. He is reluctant to share because of caution, not because the seal stops him.

**Outcome: She accepts him**, with a pointed question about his motives first.

**Why acceptance**

- **Not foreign to her:** She has already crossed worlds through the Elder Blood. A multiversal traveler is someone who finally understands her situation.
- **Kinship:** Being understood without explaining is rare for her and carries real weight.
- **Bond came first:** Her trust is action-based, and the shared danger and rescues already proved him.
- **Freedom:** The multiverse suits her need to never be caged. A partner who can move with her feels like freedom rather than a leash.

**Her reaction**

- A sharp hit of hurt at not being told, followed by a direct question: did he know what she is when they met?
- Relief once she is satisfied he did not seek her for the Elder Blood.
- Curiosity and restlessness about his worlds, and a push to see them.

**Does she sense his reservations?**

Yes. Years of being hunted trained her to read hesitation and hidden agendas. She reads his reluctance as caution, because she hides things herself. She respects it once she knows the reason is not exploitation.

**Memory seal**

She knows places leave marks on those who cross them. A seal that keeps each world fresh makes sense to her as part of how travel works. Since he carries the memories out, she treats his history as fully available to her once he chooses to share it.

**What would pull her away:** he used the multiverse to manipulate her, hid it in a way that endangered her, she learns it through exposure rather than his own disclosure, he treats her as a resource rather than a partner, or he sought her out for the Elder Blood or her power.

**What wins her:** proof he did not seek her power. After that she wants to move with him.
